# Autodesk AI

> 来源: [https://www.autodesk.com](https://www.autodesk.com)
> 抓取时间: 2026-08-25
> 公司: Autodesk

---

Autodesk AI encompasses a suite of artificial intelligence and machine learning capabilities embedded across Autodesk's design, engineering, and construction software products. These AI features span AutoCAD, Revit, Fusion, and other tools in the Autodesk ecosystem, empowering professionals in architecture, engineering, construction (AEC), and manufacturing to work more efficiently and produce better outcomes.

## Core AI Capabilities

### Generative Design
Generative Design, first introduced in Revit 2021 through the AEC Collection, uses algorithmic problem-solving to rapidly generate design alternatives based on defined goals, constraints, and inputs. Designers can explore optimized solutions for spatial planning, structural design, and building performance, presented in visual formats with responsive result graphs featuring sorting, selection, and filtering capabilities.

### Autodesk Assistant
Introduced as a Tech Preview in Revit 2027 (2026), Autodesk Assistant brings AI directly into the modeling environment. Instead of navigating complex menus or piecing together workflows manually, users can interact with their model using natural language to:
- Ask questions about their designs
- Generate documentation steps
- Trigger basic modeling actions
- Get AI-powered support for creating more and working smarter

### Machine Learning in AutoCAD
AutoCAD 2024 and later versions incorporate machine learning features that help teams unlock insights and leverage automations. These assistive ML technologies support drafting, engineering, and automating design workflows, helping professionals work more productively.

### Generative AI Integration
Autodesk combines traditional generative design (algorithm-based optimization) with generative AI (neural network-based creation) to enhance workflows. Computer-aided design began the evolution, automation technologies like generative design enhanced these workflows, and now generative AI promises to make processes even faster.

## Key Features Across Products

### Architecture, Engineering & Construction (AEC)
- **BIM Intelligence**: AI-enhanced Building Information Modeling for clash detection, quantity takeoff, and design coordination
- **Automated Drawing Generation**: Machine learning assists in creating construction documentation
- **Construction Project Insights**: Predictive analytics for project scheduling, risk assessment, and resource optimization

### Manufacturing & Product Design
- **Topology Optimization**: AI-driven material distribution for lightweight yet strong component designs
- **Performance Simulation**: Automated testing of design alternatives against multiple performance criteria
- **Cloud-Connected Workflows**: AI services that connect design to fabrication

## Technical Approach

Autodesk AI brings intelligence inside the model rather than keeping it as a separate tool. The vision for Revit 2027 emphasizes a more fluid way of working where tools support progress through AI integration — from integrated workflows with Forma Industry Cloud to elevated precision modeling and carbon insights.

## Industry Impact

Autodesk AI capabilities serve millions of professionals globally across architecture, engineering, construction, manufacturing, and media & entertainment industries. The platform's AI tools aim to automate repetitive work, generate better-performing design alternatives, and enable data-driven decision making throughout the project lifecycle.
