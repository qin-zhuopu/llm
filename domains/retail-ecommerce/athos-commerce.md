# Athos Commerce (formerly Klevu) — AI Product Discovery Platform

Athos Commerce unites Searchspring, Klevu, and Intelligent Reach under one company to transform ecommerce. It delivers ecommerce search, merchandising, personalization, and product feed management, connecting shoppers with the right products across website, marketplaces, comparison shopping engines, social channels, and AI platforms.

## One AI engine
A single AI engine powers every agent and product in the platform, turning product and shopper behavioral data into smarter, more relevant results:
- Predictive — anticipate shopper intent and demand; optimize merchandising, promotions, recommendations.
- Semantic — understand what shoppers mean (intent/context, not keyword match).
- Generative — create and enrich product content, descriptions, and answers automatically at scale.
- Multimodal — understand images and text together.

## AI Agents (purpose-built)
- Conversational Assistant — trains on the product catalog, web copy, and content to guide shoppers, respond to questions, understand intent, and accelerate purchase.
- Channel Assistant — automatically audits and improves product feeds to increase visibility on Google Shopping and expand into social commerce and marketplaces.
- GEO Assistant — enriches catalog, optimizes for Answer Engines, and syndicates to AI platforms including ChatGPT and Google Gemini.

## Built-in AI
AI woven into search (Hybrid Search: semantic AI understanding + keyword precision), merchandising, personalization, and product feed management.

## Results (self-reported)
- +951% increase in revenue per visit (Michael Stars case study)
- +15% increase in average order value

## Positioning
Type: model system + workflow application — a proprietary AI engine (predictive/semantic/generative/multimodal) plus purpose-built AI agents for product discovery across channels. Klevu (AI search/discovery) is now part of Athos Commerce.
