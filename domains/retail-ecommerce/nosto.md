# Nosto — AI-powered Commerce Experience Platform

Nosto is an AI-powered ecommerce personalization, search, and merchandising platform. It offers an integrated Commerce Experience Platform (CXP) covering product recommendations, commerce search, category merchandising, content personalization, and A/B testing, all driven by AI over a retailer's catalog and shopper behavior data.

## Huginn — agentic AI commerce
Nosto's AI agent Huginn operates 24/7 to uncover revenue opportunities, answer strategic questions, and enhance every customer interaction. It prepares brands for agentic commerce by amplifying search, merchandising, personalization, and A/B testing, and connects to a merchant's preferred tools.

## Product suite
- Product Recommendations (AI personalization). Case: generates ~9% of Marc Jacobs' online revenue.
- Commerce Search.
- Category / Merchandising with AI-assisted category merchandising.
- Content personalization (schedule and place content in templates).
- A/B testing.

## Positioning
Type: model system + workflow application — proprietary personalization/recommendation and search models plus an agentic layer (Huginn), on top of shopper behavior and catalog data. Serves DTC and enterprise retail brands.
