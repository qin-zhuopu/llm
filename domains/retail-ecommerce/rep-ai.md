# REP AI — Agentic Commerce Operating System

REP AI is an AI agentic commerce platform for growth-focused ecommerce brands, powering millions of shopper conversations. It makes a storefront proactive: the "storefront thinks, responds, and acts." REP AI detects buying intent, starts contextual conversations, guides decisions, and resolves support in real time across every channel.

## How it works
- Detects intent — monitors hundreds of behavioral signals (hesitation, comparisons, exit risk).
- Starts conversations — contextual, natural-language responses at the right moment.
- Learns and improves — each interaction becomes structured intelligence.

## One AI, six tools replaced (Agentic Commerce Operating System)
1. AI sales associate — reacts to behavior, narrows products through conversation, AI-timed complementary product recommendations, drives higher AOV via contextual upsells.
2. Support automation — resolves order tracking, returns, policies; captures recurring CX issues.
3. Helpdesk — tickets, routing, automation, unified inbox connected to the AI.
4. Insights — analyzes interactions to uncover buying friction, product demand, conversion opportunities.

## Multi-channel
Website widgets (chat, full-page, search), email engagement, social media (Instagram/Facebook DMs, comments, stories), and WhatsApp — one connected AI with consistent brand voice and product knowledge.

## Results (self-reported)
- 10-30% lift in CVR
- 50-70% reduction in support tickets
- 16%+ lift in AOV from AI-guided sales
- 97% of shopper questions answered automatically
- 5x+ ROI

## Positioning
Type: workflow application — conversational shopping/sales agent orchestrating LLMs plus behavioral-intent detection over the merchant's catalog and shopper signals. Retail/ecommerce side (guided selling + support), commonly on Shopify brands.
