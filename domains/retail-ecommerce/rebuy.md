# Rebuy — Ecommerce Personalization Platform for Shopify

Rebuy is an AI-powered ecommerce personalization platform for Shopify. It claims 50,000+ Shopify brands have boosted AOV, driven conversion, and lifted LTV with Rebuy.

## Value propositions
- Improve customer experience — over 70% of customers expect personalized experiences; Rebuy delivers relevant recommendations across the customer journey.
- Reduce churn — personalization boosts brand loyalty and reduces abandoned carts.
- Increase AOV & LTV — data-driven shopping experiences accelerate revenue growth.

## Capabilities
Rebuy's personalization engine powers Smart Cart, product recommendations, upsells and cross-sells, post-purchase offers, and search across the Shopify shopping journey, driven by shopper behavior and catalog data.

## Positioning
Type: model system + workflow application — proprietary recommendation/personalization models over Shopify shopper behavior and catalog data, deeply integrated into the Shopify commerce stack.
