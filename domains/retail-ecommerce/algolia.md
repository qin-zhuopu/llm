# Algolia — AI Search & Discovery

Algolia is a French/US search-as-a-service platform (founded 2012, Y Combinator W2014) whose AI Search product powers ecommerce product discovery. It is designed for the complexity of real user behavior — messy queries, shifting trends, and constantly changing catalogs — delivering consistent relevance at scale without constant manual tuning. For retailers it improves engagement, conversion, and reduces support costs.

## Scale and business
- 18,000+ customers, handling 1.75+ trillion searches per year (per Wikipedia / company).
- Present in 70+ data centers across 16 worldwide regions (2019).
- Funding: $18.3M Series A (2015, Accel), $53M Series B (2017, Accel), $150M Series D (2021) at a $2.25B unicorn valuation.
- Acquisitions: Morphl (2021, AI/ML for search personalization), Search.io (2022).
- ~800-1000 employees; largest team is R&D.

## Technology
Search delivered as a service via RESTful JSON APIs; data pushed from client to Algolia, search box embedded in client pages. Distributed Search Network (DSN) replicates data globally to answer queries from the nearest location. AI capabilities include NeuralSearch (vector + keyword hybrid), AI personalization, dynamic re-ranking, and query understanding.

## Positioning
Type: model system — proprietary search relevance and AI ranking/personalization models offered as an API/SaaS platform (not a general LLM). Used heavily in retail/ecommerce for site search, category ranking, and recommendations.
