# Coveo — AI Search & Conversational Product Discovery (Ecommerce)

Coveo transforms search into an intent-driven discovery experience, delivering relevant results for every query — short-head, long-tail, conversational, or GenAI-assisted. Using a host of mature AI models, Coveo personalizes experiences in real time, even for anonymous shoppers and cold-start products, supporting conversational commerce strategies.

Unlike traditional search solutions, Coveo balances relevance with ecommerce KPIs, optimizing for both customer experience and business outcomes. It integrates search, recommendations, and generative product discovery into a single platform for consistent, data-driven experiences across all touchpoints.

## Enterprise capabilities
- Built for enterprise complexity: multiple brands, currencies, regions, 50+ languages, and 50+ markets in a single environment (local and global rules).
- Unified index that standardizes and centralizes data across four lines of business: website, commerce, service, and workplace — delivering individualized, connected experiences online and offline, pre and post purchase.
- Integrates with SAP, Salesforce, and Shopify; composable platform with Atomic component library and dedicated APIs; MACH-certified.
- Security: ISO 27001-inspired governance, CoBIT-informed maturity models, ISM3-defined security processes, NIST-based measures.

## Company
Coveo Solutions Inc. is a Canadian SaaS company (Quebec City) providing e-commerce and enterprise search software. Publicly traded (TSX: CVO). Founded 2005 as a spin-off of Copernic Technologies; Louis Têtu CEO since 2008. Acquisitions: Tooso (2019, AI digital commerce engines), Qubit (2021, AI personalization for merchandising). Nov 2019 raised US$172M led by OMERS Private Equity at US$1B+ valuation.

## Positioning
Type: model system + workflow application — Coveo runs a suite of proprietary AI relevance/personalization/ranking models plus GenAI-assisted discovery orchestration over a unified enterprise index.
