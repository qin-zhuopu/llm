## Questions?

### Does using Sidekick cost money?

No. Sidekick is included with your Shopify plan. Features and usage limits vary by plan.

### How do user permissions work in Sidekick?

Every staff member can use Sidekick based on their admin permissions. It respects access controls, so team members only interact with data and features they're authorized to use.

### What makes Sidekick different from other AI tools?

Sidekick knows your business. It has direct access to your Shopify data, understands commerce workflows, and takes action in your admin. Generic AI tools can't see your store or complete tasks for you.

### Can I use Sidekick in different languages?

Yes. Sidekick adapts to your store's language settings and can help you in multiple languages. Ask questions in the language you're most comfortable with.

### How do I get started with Sidekick?

Open your Shopify admin and click the purple glasses icon. That's it. No setup, no configuration. Just start asking questions about your business and Sidekick will help.
