# Lily AI — Lily Max Product Intelligence Engine

Lily AI is a retail/ecommerce "product intelligence" company. Its flagship, Lily Max, is a goal-based agentic system that enriches a retailer's product catalog into consumer-language, schema-validated attributes so AI-mediated commerce surfaces (Google Shopping/Ads, Meta Ads, onsite search, and LLM/agentic discovery) can match and act on products. Its core thesis: "AI can't recommend what it can't understand / read."

## How Lily Max works
A goal-based (not black-box) operating model:
1. Human sets the goal — team chooses the surface, product set, success metric (e.g. Google Shopping ROAS, onsite search conversion), test cadence, and approval rules.
2. Agents execute — Lily agents evaluate the feed, PDPs, attributes, and structured data; find missing/weak signals; generate richer product intelligence; prepare variants and test design.
3. Human approves — teams review expected impact and confidence before anything ships.

The optimization loop has five steps: detect gaps → generate enrichments → launch a controlled test → measure lift → recommend what to scale. Each test makes the next smarter — a continuous experiment engine that Lily calls its moat.

## Enrichment and scoring
Lily maps merchant/retailer catalog language into consumer/shopper language and produces an "agent-ready payload" (resolvable, schema-validated). It scores product information on completeness, correctness, compliance, relevance, and differentiation.

## Measurement rigor
- Matched-spend A/B tests (enriched vs control at equal spend)
- Difference-in-differences vs pre-period baselines
- Statistical significance with confidence intervals

## Operational pace
When Google introduced Conversational Attributes in Merchant Center (a product-data format for AI Mode, Gemini, and Business Agent), Lily Max shipped support for all six attributes within days.

## Positioning
Type: model system / workflow application — goal-based AI agents for product-attribute enrichment and continuous experimentation on top of foundation models. Broader than ACO (agentic commerce optimization); positioned versus content generators, feed managers, ACO-only tools, and visibility tools.
