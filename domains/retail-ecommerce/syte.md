# Syte — Product Discovery Platform

"The World's First Product Discovery Platform." AI-Powered Product Discovery For Ecommerce — a recommendation and discovery platform built from the ground up for ecommerce, with strong visual AI roots (fashion/apparel, jewelry, home & furniture).

## Visual Discovery
- Image Search & Inspiration Gallery (camera / photo-based visual search)
- Visual Recommendation Engines: Shop Similar, Shop Social, Shop the Look/Room & more
- Advanced Personalized Recommendations
- Visual AI & Data-Driven Marketing Tools

## AI Tagging & Merchandising
Drive revenue, boost text search, and optimize merchandising with automatic product tagging:
- AI Deep Tags (automatic product attribute tagging from images)
- Deep Tag Analytics
- Thematic Tags
- AI Tag Editor & Lexicon Manager

## Outcomes (self-reported)
Apparel retailers using Syte recommendations report higher CVR, AOV, and ARPU. Positioned for verticals with strong visual attributes (fashion/apparel, jewelry, home & furniture).

## Positioning
Type: model system — Syte trains proprietary computer-vision models for visual search and automatic attribute tagging over merchant catalogs, layered with recommendation and merchandising models.
