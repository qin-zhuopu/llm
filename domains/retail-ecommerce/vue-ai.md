# Vue.ai — Enterprise AI Orchestration Platform (retail/ecommerce)

Vue.ai positions itself as an "Enterprise AI Orchestration Platform" — a single AI Orchestrator accountable for AI transformation, with a composable, modular, AI-native architecture. It offers fast onboarding and go-live, with automatic data cleaning and enrichment across use cases.

## Vue.ai Platform
- Data Self-Prep AI — connect, ingest, clean, enrich messy data.
- Adaptive Automation — guided straight-through processing, machine-first.
- Composable Workflows — drag-and-drop low-code interface, preset templates.
- Self-Learning AI and Active Learning — federated models that respond to contextual shifts and adapt with user behavior.
- Quick Bootstrap with Little Data — train models with smaller data samples quickly.
- High Dimensional Graphs — rich customer, product, and process graphs.

## eCommerce & Consumer use cases
Product tagging, content moderation, catalog assortment visualization, automated on-model imagery, excess inventory management, thematic product curations, personalized eCommerce & marketing journeys, virtual dressing rooms, product recommendations, customer loyalty program personalization, inventory demand prediction & forecasting, channel optimization, churn prediction.

## Company & positioning
Vue.ai has roots in retail image AI (product tagging, on-model imagery, virtual dressing rooms) and also serves Financial Services, Insurance, Staffing & Logistics. Partnerships include Google Cloud Partner Advantage and Meta (inclusive AI models). 96% of customers see Vue.ai as a "strategic partner"; a 30:60:90 rollout framework (Day 30 pilot go-live, Day 60 ROI proof, Day 90 scale).

Type: model system + workflow application — Vue.ai trains proprietary computer-vision and self-learning/federated models (product tagging, on-model imagery, virtual try-on) and orchestrates them through composable workflows.
