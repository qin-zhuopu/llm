# Bloomreach Loomi AI

Loomi AI is Bloomreach's agentic AI platform — the centralized "brain" powering every Bloomreach product (Discovery / ecommerce search, Engagement / marketing, Content, and Ads). It unifies customer data, predictive models, and channel activation in a single platform so a signal from one channel (e.g. search) can personalize another (e.g. email) in milliseconds. Bloomreach positions Loomi AI as the only intelligence engine that unifies data, context, and real-time execution.

## Loomi Connect
Loomi Connect lets brands build custom AI agents grounded entirely in their own catalog, personalization, and business rules. It extends Bloomreach intelligence into external AI tools, starting with a ChatGPT beta that brings the complete catalog into the chat interface to deliver personalized product recommendations governed by exact merchandising rules — i.e. positioning for agentic commerce.

## Bloomreach Discovery (Ecommerce Search)
Powered by Loomi AI, ecommerce search "doesn't just react — it thinks, anticipates, initiates conversations, explains reasoning, and gets smarter with every interaction." It shifts merchandisers from manually fixing search to strategic oversight of a self-driving revenue engine. Captures every search, purchase, click, return, and preference as first-party data to personalize for every shopper, going beyond clickstream data. A shopping agent proactively offers real-time, personalized assistance on-site, turning passive browsing into high-converting two-way conversations.

## Positioning
- Type: workflow application + model system — orchestrates first-party retailer data, predictive models, and agents on top of foundation models; product-specific personalization and merchandising models.
- Customers referenced: Canadian Tire (site search & personalization), among enterprise retailers/brands.
