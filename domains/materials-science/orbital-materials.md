## Orbital Industries - The First "AI Industrial"

Orbital Materials (operating as Orbital Industries) is a company founded in late 2022 that combines frontier AI with physical product development. They describe themselves as the first "AI Industrial" - companies that sell physical products but operate like software companies.

### Core Philosophy

The company believes AI is the best shot at solving the challenge of producing more with less, as global GDP per capita sits at roughly a sixth of America's and the working-age population is shrinking. Their approach: speeding up the invention and manufacture of physical products through AI.

### Key Technology Areas

#### Multi-Scale World Models

AI-accelerated simulators spanning quantum physics through fluid dynamics, enabling iteration in silico before committing to the real world. Their first models - for advanced materials - are described as world leading. These models cover:
- Quantum physics simulations
- Molecular dynamics
- Fluid dynamics
- Cross-scale property prediction

#### Autoresearch Agents (Sample Efficient)

Building a pilot plant or synthesising a new material doesn't give many shots on goal - each experiment costs months and millions. Orbital's autoresearch agents are designed to be sample efficient: finding the right answer in as few experiments as possible.

Key characteristics:
- Optimized for physical industries where experiments are expensive
- Sample-efficient exploration strategies
- Integration with robotic labs and physical engineering sites

### Business Model: AI Industrial

Orbital believes AI enables new types of hardware businesses. Traditional hardware R&D has huge, siloed departments split across engineering disciplines. AI-native industrial companies will look more like frontier AI labs:
- Small, interdisciplinary teams
- Specialized AI agents for design
- Robotic labs for automated experimentation
- Physical engineering sites for manufacturing
- Ship better products faster, at a fraction of R&D cost

### Commercial Products

#### GPU Cooling Materials (Data Centers)

Their first commercial application is in AI data centers, where they have:
- Discovered entirely new molecular classes for high-density GPU cooling
- Developed commercial data center cooling products
- Demonstrated the AI Industrial model with real revenue-generating products

### Future Ambitions

The company's ambitions extend well beyond data centers to:
- Energy sector materials
- Semiconductor materials
- Materials and machines the broader economy is built from

### Team Structure

The company operates with:
- A frontier AI team pushing state of the art in physical AI
- Materials, hardware and manufacturing teams using those models
- Small, talent-dense teams (inspired by frontier AI labs)

### Vision on Superintelligence

Orbital holds the view that AI Industrials may be the best path to superintelligence. Today's AI learns by mimicking human language, then training on computer-verifiable tasks (math, code). But the universe itself isn't fully verifiable on a computer - experimental results that surprise and confound us provide the physical reward signals that more advanced AI needs. These signals will be generated at AI Industrials.
