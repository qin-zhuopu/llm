## Atinary SDLabs: Self-Driving Labs Pioneer and Platform

Atinary Technologies is a pioneer in Self-Driving Labs (SDLabs), offering an AI + robotics closed-loop experimentation platform that can compress years of R&D into weeks. The company's platform integrates artificial intelligence with automated laboratory robotics to enable autonomous experimental optimization.

### Core Technology

The Atinary SDLabs platform provides:
- **AI-Driven Experimental Design**: Bayesian optimization and machine learning algorithms that intelligently select the next experiments to run
- **Robotic Automation**: Integration with laboratory robotics for hands-free experiment execution
- **Closed-Loop Optimization**: Continuous feedback between AI predictions and experimental results
- **No-Code Deployment**: AI can be deployed in as little as 2 hours without programming knowledge

### Performance Claims

- **5-100x faster** than traditional Design of Experiments (DOE) and One-Factor-At-A-Time (OFAT) methods
- **50%+ cost reduction** in R&D experimentation
- **2-hour deployment** time for AI-guided experiments without coding

### Founding and Vision

Atinary was founded with the vision of making Self-Driving Labs accessible to all researchers, regardless of their programming expertise. The platform democratizes AI-driven experimentation by providing intuitive interfaces that require no coding skills.
