## Kebotix: Self-Driving Lab + Enterprise AI for Materials Discovery

Kebotix is a materials science AI company that combines artificial intelligence, physics-based modeling, and advanced automation into a unified cloud platform for accelerated materials discovery. The company provides enterprise AI solutions that enable organizations to dramatically reduce R&D timelines for new materials development.

### Core Technology

Kebotix operates on a closed-loop design paradigm called **Predict-Produce-Prove**:

1. **Predict**: AI and physics-based models generate candidate materials with targeted properties
2. **Produce**: Automated self-driving laboratories synthesize the predicted materials
3. **Prove**: Experimental validation confirms material properties, feeding data back into the AI models

This closed-loop approach eliminates the traditional trial-and-error methodology in materials research, enabling faster convergence to optimal solutions.

### Platform Capabilities

- **Self-Driving Laboratory**: Fully automated experimental workflows that can run 24/7 without human intervention
- **AI-Powered Prediction**: Machine learning models trained on materials data to predict properties and suggest novel compositions
- **Physics-Based Modeling**: Integration of first-principles simulations with data-driven approaches
- **Cloud Platform**: Enterprise-grade cloud infrastructure for collaborative materials R&D
- **Custom Solutions**: Tailored materials discovery pipelines for specific client needs

### Business Model

Kebotix provides customized materials discovery and R&D digitalization services to enterprise clients. The platform is designed to:
- Accelerate the pace of materials innovation
- Reduce the cost of experimental iterations
- Enable data-driven decision making in R&D
- Digitize and standardize materials research workflows

### Applications

The platform serves a broad range of materials discovery challenges including:
- Advanced polymers and coatings
- Battery materials and energy storage
- Catalysts for chemical processes
- Specialty chemicals and formulations
