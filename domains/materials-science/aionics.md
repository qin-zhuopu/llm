## Aionics: AI + Physics Simulation for New Materials Formulation Design

Aionics is a materials science AI company that leverages artificial intelligence and physics simulation to design new material formulations. The company focuses on high-impact applications including electric vehicle batteries, aviation batteries, long-duration energy storage, and green manufacturing.

### Core Technology

Aionics' platform combines:
- **AI-Driven Screening**: Ability to screen billions of formulation candidates to identify the most promising ones
- **Physics Simulation**: First-principles and molecular dynamics simulations to validate AI predictions
- **New Molecule Design**: Generative AI for designing novel molecular structures with targeted properties
- **Supply Chain Integration**: Connecting material discoveries with supply chain sourcing for practical deployment

### Key Applications

**Electric Vehicle Batteries:**
- Next-generation electrolyte formulations
- High-energy-density cathode materials
- Fast-charging anode materials

**Aviation Batteries:**
- Ultra-lightweight, high-energy materials for aerospace applications
- Safety-optimized battery chemistries

**Long-Duration Energy Storage:**
- Grid-scale storage materials
- Flow battery electrolytes
- Solid-state battery materials

**Green Manufacturing:**
- Sustainable material alternatives
- Reduced environmental footprint formulations
- Recyclable material designs

**Consumer Electronics:**
- Compact, high-performance battery materials
- Thermal management materials

### Approach

The platform screens tens of billions of possible formulations to find the best candidates, dramatically reducing the time and cost of traditional Edisonian approaches to materials development. By combining AI predictions with physics-based validation, Aionics ensures that recommended formulations are both theoretically sound and practically synthesizable.

### Customers

Aionics serves clients across the EV, aviation, grid energy storage, and consumer electronics industries, helping them accelerate their materials R&D pipelines from years to weeks.
