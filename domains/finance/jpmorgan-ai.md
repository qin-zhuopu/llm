### AI Agents & Hybrid Reasoning

To automate multi-step multi-agent tasks, involving computer-use actions, ask-for-help and learn from human input, leading to efficiency and satisfaction.

### AI Planning & Knowledge Management

To optimize financial strategy by using AI planning to simulate various scenarios and outcomes, enabling more informed decision-making.

### AI Optimization & Decision Making

To optimize financial processes by leveraging AI to identify inefficiencies and implement cost-effective solutions.

### Foundation Models for the Financial Domain

To develop foundational models tailored for the finance domain, enabling more accurate and context-specific financial predictions and analyses.

### Synthetic Data, Time Series & Behavior Analysis

To generate synthetic data, model behaviors, and analyze time series data for financial processes.

[Learn more about Synthetic Data](/about/technology/research/ai/synthetic-data)

### AI Multimodal Document Processing & Analysis

To automate the extraction, interpretation, and generation of financial information, increasing efficiency and accuracy in financial operations.

### AI Trust, Transparency & Safety

To enhance trust in financial services by providing clear, understandable, and recourse-based AI-driven decision-making processes.

### IndexGPT

2023年5月，JPMorgan Chase申请了"IndexGPT"商标，该产品利用"颠覆性的人工智能技术"和云计算软件为客户选择投资组合。这表明摩根大通正在开发基于大语言模型的投资顾问产品，用于个性化投资推荐和投资组合优化。

### DocLLM

JPMorgan AI Research 开发的文档理解大模型，专注于多模态金融文档处理，能够理解和提取金融报告、合同和监管文件中的结构化信息。

### 七大AI研究方向

1. **AI Agents & Hybrid Reasoning** - 多智能体多步骤任务自动化，结合computer-use和人机协作
2. **AI Planning & Knowledge Management** - 金融策略规划与场景模拟优化
3. **AI Optimization & Decision Making** - 金融流程效率优化与成本控制
4. **Foundation Models for Financial Domain** - 金融垂直领域基础模型
5. **Synthetic Data, Time Series & Behavior Analysis** - 合成数据与时序行为建模
6. **AI Multimodal Document Processing** - 多模态文档自动化处理
7. **AI Trust, Transparency & Safety** - AI可信度与安全保障

### 技术规模
- 全球AI/ML领域拥有2,000+数据科学家和工程师
- 每年AI研究预算超过100亿美元（Technology & Operations总预算部分）
- 已部署300+个AI用例覆盖风控、交易、运营等核心业务

### AlgoCRYPT Center of Excellence
专注于密码学和隐私计算在金融AI中的应用研究。

### TrustAI Center of Excellence
研究AI系统的可解释性、公平性和鲁棒性，确保金融AI决策的透明度和合规性。
