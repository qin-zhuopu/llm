Palantir AIP (Artificial Intelligence Platform) is an enterprise-grade generative AI platform that powers real-time, AI-driven decision-making in the most critical commercial and government contexts around the world. AIP enables developers and builders to create LLM-backed workflows, agents, and applications using the Palantir platform's comprehensive suite of AI-native tools.

## Core Capabilities

### AIP Chatbot Studio (formerly AIP Agent Studio)
AIP Chatbot Studio provides a development environment for building conversational AI agents that can interact with enterprise data through the Palantir Ontology. These chatbots can answer complex questions, trigger workflows, and provide insights grounded in organizational context.

### AIP Logic
AIP Logic enables the creation of LLM-driven functions and automations that can be integrated into enterprise workflows. It allows organizations to define structured AI operations with clear inputs, outputs, and governance controls.

### Ontology-Based Context Integration
A key differentiator of Palantir AIP is its integration with the Palantir Ontology — a semantic layer that continuously integrates and organizes enterprise data into meaningful objects, relationships, and actions. This provides LLMs with rich, structured context about the organization's data landscape, enabling more accurate and relevant AI outputs.

### Security and Governance
AIP provides robust access controls, encryption, and auditing capabilities to maintain data integrity and transparency. Built-in governance tools help organizations maintain accountability and historical lineage in AI operations, ensuring compliance with regulatory requirements.

## Architecture

AIP operates through the "k-LLM" paradigm, providing secure connectivity to a wide range of large language models and multimodal models. The platform architecture includes:

- **Secure LLM Connectivity**: Connects to the full range of LLMs while maintaining enterprise security standards
- **Agent Development Toolchain**: Comprehensive tools for building, testing, and deploying AI agents
- **Evals Framework**: A comprehensive evaluation system for governing AI workflows in production and measuring ongoing performance
- **AI-Enabled Applications**: Tools like Pipeline Builder and Workshop that leverage AIP capabilities

## Use Cases

Palantir AIP has been deployed across diverse industries including public health, battery production, defense operations, supply chain optimization, and process mining. Organizations use AIP to build custom AI applications for compliance, automation, and operational decision-making at scale.

## Platform Integration

AIP is part of the broader Palantir platform ecosystem that includes Foundry (data integration and analytics) and Apollo (deployment and management infrastructure). Together, these platforms provide end-to-end capabilities for organizations to leverage AI safely and effectively in their enterprises.
