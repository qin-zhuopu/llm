Baker Hughes AI encompasses a comprehensive suite of artificial intelligence and machine learning solutions designed for the oil & gas and broader energy industry. Through partnerships with C3 AI (forming the BHC3 joint venture) and proprietary technology development, Baker Hughes delivers enterprise-grade AI applications that address critical operational challenges across the energy value chain.

## Core AI Solutions

### Predictive Maintenance
Baker Hughes' predictive maintenance solutions leverage machine learning to forecast equipment failures before they occur:
- **BHC3 Reliability Application**: Predicts pump failures 30 to 50 days in advance, enabling optimized maintenance crew deployment and replacement part preparation
- **Neural Network Models**: Analyze nearly one terabyte of sensor data to predict machine failures, with projected savings exceeding $10 million
- **Control Valve Failure Prediction**: Integrates five years of historical time-series data with asset hierarchy and work order notifications to predict valve failures

### Drilling Optimization
- **I-Trak Drilling Automation**: Meets directional drilling targets while optimizing parameters for high penetration rate and low wellbore tortuosity
- **i-Trak Automated ROP Optimization**: Reduces excessive vibrations and other drilling dysfunctions while maximizing rate of penetration
- **TRU-ARMS Predict**: Advanced lookahead and geostopping services that reduce seismic uncertainty, enabling earlier detection and more confident drilling decisions

### Reservoir Modeling
AI-enhanced reservoir characterization tools that combine geological data, seismic information, and production history to create more accurate models of subsurface formations, improving recovery predictions and well placement optimization.

### Emissions Monitoring
AI-powered solutions for detecting, measuring, and reducing greenhouse gas emissions across oil and gas operations, supporting the industry's energy transition goals and regulatory compliance requirements.

### Equipment Health Diagnostics
Real-time monitoring and AI-driven diagnostics for critical energy equipment including pumps, compressors, turbines, and rotating machinery, enabling condition-based maintenance strategies.

## Technology Platform

### Leucipa™ Automated Field Production
Baker Hughes' Leucipa platform uses artificial intelligence-powered workflows to improve operational efficiencies across diverse portfolios of producing wells. Recently deployed across US natural gas fields in the Marcellus, Utica, and Haynesville shales.

### Enterprise AI Architecture
The BHC3 partnership provides an enterprise AI platform that enables:

- Rapid application development and deployment
- Continuous optimization of AI models over time
- Integration of multiple data sources into unified data models
