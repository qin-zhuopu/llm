## Deep Origin: Physics AI Drug Discovery Platform

Deep Origin is a physics AI drug discovery platform addressing the fundamental challenge that 90% of drugs fail in clinical trials. The company builds multi-scale predictive models spanning from quantum mechanics to cellular biology, enabling more accurate prediction of drug behavior in living systems.

### Core Technology

**Multi-Scale Predictive Models:**
- Quantum-to-cell modeling approach
- First-principles physics simulations integrated with AI
- Bridging molecular interactions to cellular-level effects
- Addressing the root causes of clinical trial failures

### Therapeutic Data Commons Rankings

Deep Origin has achieved #1 rankings on the Therapeutic Data Commons leaderboard across multiple critical ADMET properties:
- **Permeability**: Predicting drug absorption
- **Lipophilicity**: Predicting drug distribution
- **Solubility**: Predicting drug formulation viability
- **Half-life**: Predicting drug metabolism
- **hERG**: Predicting cardiac toxicity risk
- **AMES**: Predicting mutagenicity risk

### Pipeline and Results

**CD73 Project:**
- 159 compounds evaluated
- 30% hit rate achieved
- Demonstrates platform's ability to generate high-quality drug candidates

### Funding and Backing

- $50M+ initial funding
- Additional $32M raised
- ARPA-H (Advanced Research Projects Agency for Health) contract
- Strong investor confidence in physics-AI approach

### Founding Team

Founded by **Michael Antonov**, co-creator of Oculus (acquired by Meta for $2 billion). Antonov brings deep expertise in physics simulation and computational systems from building VR technology.

### Team

- 100+ scientists across physics, chemistry, biology, and AI
- Multidisciplinary approach combining domain expertise with AI capabilities
- Focused on solving the translational gap between lab and clinic

### Why Physics AI?

Traditional AI drug discovery relies heavily on statistical pattern matching from historical data. Deep Origin's physics-based approach:
- Models fundamental molecular interactions from first principles
- Generalizes better to novel chemical space
- Provides mechanistic understanding of drug behavior
- Predicts properties in biological contexts more accurately
