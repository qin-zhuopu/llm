## Iambic Therapeutics: AI Molecular Superintelligence Platform

Iambic Therapeutics is an AI-first drug discovery company that has built a molecular superintelligence platform to dramatically accelerate the drug development process. The company integrates cutting-edge AI technologies with high-throughput experimental capabilities to design and optimize drug molecules years faster than traditional approaches.

### Core AI Technologies

**Enchant (Multi-Modal Transformer):**
- Multi-modal transformer model that integrates diverse molecular data types
- Processes chemical structures, biological assay data, and structural information simultaneously
- Enables unified molecular understanding across modalities

**NeuralPLexer (Co-Folding AI):**
- Advanced AI model for predicting protein-ligand co-folding structures
- Predicts how drug molecules interact with their target proteins
- Enables structure-based drug design with unprecedented accuracy

### High-Throughput Experimental Platform

Iambic operates an integrated experimental platform that enables:
- Weekly cycles from new molecule design to biological data generation
- Rapid synthesis and testing of AI-designed molecules
- Continuous feedback loop between AI predictions and experimental validation
- Scale and speed that far exceeds traditional medicinal chemistry workflows

### Drug Pipeline

**IAM1363 (HER2 Brain-Penetrant Inhibitor):**
- Phase 1/1b clinical trial
- Brain-penetrant small molecule targeting HER2
- Designed for brain metastases from HER2-positive cancers
- Addresses unmet need for CNS-active HER2 therapies

**IAM217 (KIF18A Inhibitor):**
- Targeting KIF18A for ovarian cancer
- Preclinical/early clinical development
- Novel mechanism of action

**IAM-C1 (CDK2/4 Inhibitor):**
- Dual CDK2/4 inhibitor for breast cancer
- Designed to overcome CDK4/6 inhibitor resistance
- Novel selectivity profile

### Speed Advantage

The Iambic platform delivers drug candidates years faster than the industry standard by:
- Reducing hit-to-lead timelines from months to weeks
- Leveraging AI to explore vast chemical spaces efficiently
- Integrating experimental feedback in real-time
- Eliminating traditional sequential optimization bottlenecks

## 补充研究信息

> 来源: https://www.iambic.ai/platform 及 https://www.iambic.ai/pipeline
> 日期: 2025-06-18

### Enchant 技术细节
- State-of-the-art multimodal transformer，能从小型噪声数据集学习
- 跨广泛靶点类别和药物分子类型进行预测
- 突破临床前发现与临床开发之间的"数据墙"

### NeuralPLexer 技术细节
- 提供近乎即时的蛋白-配体结构洞察
- 覆盖广泛蛋白类别和药物分子
- 支持 de novo 蛋白-配体结构预测和隐性口袋(cryptic pocket)发现

### 实验平台详情
- 高通量化学：每周纳米级合成数千种独特化合物
- 高通量生物学：生成生化、代谢和细胞活性数据
- 自动化数据管线和模型重训练，每周完成闭环
- 并行测试多个替代靶标产品特征(profile)

### IAM1363 详细信息
- 口服、高选择性、泛突变、脑穿透小分子抑制剂
- 选择性靶向HER2和HER2突变体，同时保留EGFR（>1000倍体内选择性）
- 有利PK和安全性，优先肿瘤富集，有效CNS穿透
- 在多种HER2肿瘤模型（含颅内模型）中展示抗肿瘤活性
- 优于现有TKI和ADC基准
- 从项目启动到Phase 1约2年（vs 行业平均约6年）

### 平台能力范围
- 治疗领域：广泛适用，跨多种治疗领域
- 蛋白类别：跨广泛蛋白类别验证
- 作用机制：正构抑制剂、变构抑制剂、蛋白-蛋白相互作用调节剂
