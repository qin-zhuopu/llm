# Gatik — The Business of Autonomous Freight

Source: https://www.gatik.ai/
Fetched: 2026-08-27

## Positioning
"The Business of Autonomous Freight." Gatik runs driverless middle-mile freight networks daily for Fortune 50 retailers and grocers across the U.S. and Canada. Live operations dashboard shows real trips refreshed every 3 hours (box trucks running fixed short-haul routes between distribution centers and stores).

## Notes
Gatik's autonomy stack (perception, prediction, planning) is purpose-built for the constrained, repeatable middle-mile B2B short-haul use case, enabling fully driverless operations at scale. Customers include Walmart, Kroger, PepsiCo.
