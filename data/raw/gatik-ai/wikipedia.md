# Gatik — Wikipedia

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gatik
Fetched: 2026-08-27

Gatik is an autonomous trucking and delivery company operating in the United States and Canada. The company creates Level 4 autonomous trucking technology for vehicles making middle-mile commercial deliveries. Headquartered in Mountain View, California, with offices in Arkansas, Fort Worth (Texas) and Toronto. Founded 2017 by Gautam Narang (CEO), Arjun Narang, and Apeksha Kumavat.

- 2019: launched partnership with Walmart, a 1.8-mile route between a fulfillment center and the retailer's Bentonville, Arkansas location.
- By 2021: vehicles went driverless, safety driver moving to passenger seat. By November 2021, operating 21 vehicles.
- January 2020: deployed Canada's first autonomous delivery fleet with Loblaw in Ontario.
- Now used by Kroger and Tyson Foods.
- Gatik uses Level 4 autonomous trucks for the "middle mile", operating without a safety driver on repeated routes.
- Partnerships: Isuzu (N-Series truck), Cummins (powertrain/drive-by-wire), Goodyear (data-collecting tires, 2024).
- July 2025: NVIDIA technology integrated into Gatik's next-generation simulation platform for higher-fidelity simulation.
