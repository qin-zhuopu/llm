# Lily AI — How Lily Max Works (Agentic Product Intelligence)

Source: https://www.lily.ai/lily-max (fetched 2026-08-27)

Lily Max turns product intelligence into measurable lift. "Your team sets the goal. Lily agents execute the work. Your team makes the final decision."

## Operating model (goal-based system, not a black box)
1. Human sets the goal — performance teams decide what matters first, from Google Shopping ROAS to onsite search conversion; choose the surface and product set; set success metric and test cadence; define approval and deployment rules.
2. Agents execute the work — Lily agents evaluate the feed, PDPs, attributes, and structured data against the surface-specific goal; find missing/weak product signals; generate richer product intelligence; prepare variants and test design.
3. Human approves what ships — teams get a clear readout with expected impact, confidence, and next step.

## Optimization loop (five steps)
Detect gaps → generate enrichments → launch a controlled test → measure the lift → recommend what to scale. Each test makes the next one smarter as results feed back.

The moat is the system: a continuous experiment engine that learns from every surface, test, and customer.

## Operational pace
When Google introduced Conversational Attributes in Merchant Center (a new product-data format built for AI Mode, Gemini, and Business Agent), Lily Max implemented support for all six attributes within days.

## Proof / measurement rigor
- Matched-spend A/B: compare enriched and control feeds at equivalent spend levels.
- Difference-in-differences: validate lift against pre-period baselines and control groups.
- Statistical significance: report confidence and methodology footnotes.

Lily Max scores product information on completeness, correctness, compliance, relevance, and differentiation. Covers paid, organic, onsite, and agentic commerce surfaces (Google, Meta, LLM, and retailer-owned systems). Starts with Google Merchant Center (GMC) feed. Broader than ACO (agentic commerce optimization); positioned versus content generators, feed managers, ACO-only tools, and visibility tools.
