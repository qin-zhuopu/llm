Lily Max | Product Intelligence Engine for AI Commerce. "AI can't recommend what it can't understand." Lily Max agents enrich your existing product catalog, launch controlled tests, and improve performance across Google Ads, Meta Ads, AI Discovery & Agentic Commerce and Onsite.

Today, your customer is shopping across every AI surface.

## Agent-Ready Payload / PDP Enrichment
"AI can't recommend what it can't read." What an agent can pull from a live PDP today is mostly blanks; Lily enriches the same product into a resolvable, schema-validated payload an AI surface can match and act on.

Lily uses a Product Attributes engine that adds, removes, and enriches fields inline — mapping merchant/retailer catalog language into consumer/shopper language. Fields have Original vs Enriched values with scores.

## Measurement
Enriching data with Lily Max improves how products show up in Google's AI Performance Insights, surfaces the search terms that matter for each product, and runs A/B tests that prove the lift. Includes gap analysis (% of sample products tagged for each shopping occasion) before/after enrichment.
