# Plantix — Wikipedia

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plantix
Fetched: 2026-08-27

Plantix is a mobile crop advisory app for farmers, extension workers and gardeners, developed by PEAT GmbH, a Berlin-based AI startup. Released 2015 (Android). The app diagnoses plant damage (caused by pests, diseases or nutrient deficiencies) based on digital images of symptoms, then suggests pesticides/herbicides and connects users with local sellers (taking a commission on app-generated pesticide sales).

- Launched 2015 by PEAT GmbH.
- April 2020: PEAT acquired the Swiss-Indian startup Salesbee.
- Featured in BBC, Fortune, Wired, MIT Technology Review and Nature.
- Awards: CeBIT Innovation Award, USAID digital smart farming award, World Summit Award (UN).
- 2023: acquired by chemical marketing company Helm AG.
- Cooperates with international research institutes: ICRISAT, CIMMYT, CABI.
- Note: Wired (2024) reported the app's pesticide-reduction claims are challenged given its input-sales business model.
