# Plantix (PEAT GmbH) — Company History (Wikipedia)

> Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plantix
> Fetched: 2026-08-30
> Type: encyclopedia (secondary, aggregates BBC/Fortune/Wired/MIT Tech Review/Nature coverage)

Plantix is a mobile crop advisory app for farmers, extension workers and gardeners, developed by PEAT GmbH, a Berlin-based AI startup. The app diagnoses plant damage (pests, diseases, nutrient deficiencies) from digital images of symptoms, then suggests pesticides/herbicides and connects users with local sellers (taking a commission on app-generated pesticide sales).

## Milestones

- 2015: PEAT GmbH launched the Plantix app (Android).
- Apr 2020: PEAT acquired the Swiss-Indian startup Salesbee.
- 2023: PEAT/Plantix was acquired by the chemical marketing company Helm AG.

## Recognition & partners

- Featured in BBC, Fortune, Wired, MIT Technology Review, and Nature.
- Awards: CeBIT Innovation Award, USAID digital smart farming award, World Summit Award (United Nations).
- Cooperates with international research institutes: ICRISAT, CIMMYT, CABI.

## Business model note

The company's business model depends on commissions from pesticide sales generated via the app; claims of reducing environmentally harmful pesticide use have been challenged (Wired) due to this industry-dependent model.
