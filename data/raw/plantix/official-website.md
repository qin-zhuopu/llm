## Plantix | #1 FREE app for crop diagnosis and treatments

Plantix helps farmers diagnose and treat crop problems, improve productivity and
provide farming knowledge.

- Diagnose your sick crop: Take a photo of your sick crop and get a free diagnosis
  and treatment suggestions in a few seconds.
- Get expert advice: A community of agri-experts helps answer questions.
- Crop library: Information on specific crop diseases and prevention methods.

### Plantix in numbers
- The most downloaded ag-tech app worldwide.
- Answered more than 100 million crop-related questions from farmers.

Testimonials from farmers and agronomists in Brazil, Spain and Indonesia praise the
disease identification accuracy and both chemical and biological treatment
recommendations. Plantix is developed by PEAT GmbH (Berlin, Germany).
