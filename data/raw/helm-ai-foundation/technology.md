# Helm.ai — Deep Neural Network (DNN) and Generative AI Innovations (technology page)

Source: https://helm.ai/technology
Fetched: 2026-08-27

Architecture Built for Data Efficiency and Global Generalization. Helm.ai's AI architecture leverages Factored Embodied AI, Deep Teaching™, and Semantic Simulation to achieve production-ready systems with orders-of-magnitude less data. High-end Level 2+ today, unlocking Level 3 and Level 4 with the same software architecture.

Factored Embodied AI: Decoupling Perception and Policy. A "factored" approach separates Perception (seeing the world) from Policy (deciding how to drive). Extracting the geometric structure of the world before teaching a vehicle how to drive replicates the human ability to generalize.
Triple Dividend: Efficiency of understanding (Helm.ai Vision learns from vast raw video to master geometry and physics), Computational efficiency (training the planner in "Semantic Space" rather than raw pixels), Interpretability (factored architecture isolates the cause of a failure for safety certification).

Deep Teaching™: proprietary unsupervised learning method enabling training of large-scale foundation models on massive volumes of raw, unlabeled real-world driving data, overcoming the "data wall." Advantages: unbounded scalability, corner case resolution, global generalization.

Semantic Simulation: training the AI planner directly in Semantic Space. The perception engine converts the world into clean geometric representations, skipping photorealistic pixel rendering for policy training. The visual "reality gap" vanishes.

The World Model: moves beyond seeing to anticipating. Predicting Intent: projects "ghost trails" to anticipate future moves of others. Generative Flywheel: AI "dreams up" infinite rare scenarios and corner cases, a self-improving loop.

Broad Applications: universal backbone for autonomy — mining, construction, industrial robotics.

Safety compliance: ISO 26262 Functional Safety, ISO/PAS 21448 SOTIF, ASPICE.
