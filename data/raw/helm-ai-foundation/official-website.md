# Helm.ai — AI-First Software and Simulation for ADAS to Autonomous Driving (official website)

Source: https://helm.ai/
Fetched: 2026-08-27

Comprehensive AI solutions for real-time deployment and offline training and validation. Helm.ai builds AI models for real-time deployment and large-scale simulation. Trained using Deep Teaching™ and generative AI, the models enable faster, more scalable, and cost-effective development for production-bound projects across ADAS to Level 4 systems.

DEEP TEACHING™: Helm.ai's proprietary unsupervised learning method. It combines real-world data, deep learning, and applied math to train adaptable, high-performance foundation models at scale.

Foundation models trained with Deep Teaching™ and generative AI: DNN-based foundation models trained using Deep Teaching™ on large-scale real-world datasets and enhanced with generative AI. They adapt automatically to new geographies and unseen driving conditions, while fine-tuning enables efficient resolution of rare and complex corner cases.

Capabilities: Urban and highway pilot; Corner case resolution; Adaptation to regional driving conditions.

A unified approach from Level 2 to Level 4 autonomous driving built on Deep Teaching™ and novel generative AI DNN architectures.

Selected customers: Honda Motor, Volkswagen. Helm.ai partners with global automakers on production-bound projects.
