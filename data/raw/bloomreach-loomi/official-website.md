## Loomi AI
Loomi AI is the agentic platform powering every Bloomreach product. It is the centralized brain that brings customer data, predictive models, and channel activation into a single place, empowering personalization across every channel — from email to search, ads to app.

When channels share the same AI brain, results compound. A signal from search can personalize an email in milliseconds, not days. The more channels you connect, the smarter every experience gets.

Loomi AI is described as "the only intelligence engine that unifies" data, context, and real-time execution. It powers smarter personalization, automation, reporting, segmentation, and insights across the Bloomreach suite.

### Loomi Connect
Loomi Connect gives the power to build custom AI agents grounded entirely in your own catalog, personalization, and business rules. From product discovery to campaign creation, Loomi Connect extends Bloomreach intelligence into AI tools that teams and customers already use — starting with a ChatGPT beta that brings the complete catalog directly into the chat interface to deliver personalized product recommendations governed by exact merchandising rules.
