# Pando — AI-Powered TMS for Enterprises

Source: https://www.pando.ai/
Fetched: 2026-08-27

## Positioning
"Unify your entire freight lifecycle on an automated, AI-powered TMS platform."

## Core modules
- **Freight Procurement Intelligence (Negotiate Better)**: bring structure and data insights to RFQs, carrier bidding, and contract management. Benchmark rates, adapt to market shifts, and lock in contracts that hold before volatility hits your P&L.
- **Multi-modal Transportation Management System (Dispatch Better)**: plan, dispatch, and track domestic, international, and EXIM shipments across all modes on a single platform. Real-time visibility, automated carrier coordination, zero blind spots.
- **Carrier Settlement (Control Costs)**: eliminate payment leakages by matching every invoice line to contract rates and accessorial terms. Pay carriers faster, unlock invoice discounting.

Pando is an enterprise TMS company that has raised venture funding (Series B led by Iron Pillar / Chiratae). It applies AI agents across freight procurement, transportation management, and freight audit & payment/settlement for global manufacturers and CPG/enterprise shippers.
