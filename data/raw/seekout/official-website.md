SeekOut - Agentic AI Recruiting Platform | Source, Screen & Engage Talent.

Two paths:
- SeekOut Recruit (AI Recruiting Platform): AI in your recruiters' hands. Source, screen, and engage talent at scale. Combine outbound sourcing, inbound evaluation, and personalized outreach in one platform.
- SeekOut Spot (AI Recruiting Service): AI + experts augmenting your recruiting team. AI agents find, reach, and evaluate candidates while expert SeekOut recruiters guide/refine every step.

Trusted by 750+ customers. 1B+ profiles. < 2 min search setup. Same day engagement.

Capabilities:
- Inbound Evaluation: process thousands of inbound applicants in hours. AI evaluates candidates against your criteria and delivers qualified shortlists.
- Intelligent Search: go beyond keywords to find hidden talent. Search 1B+ profiles across external sources and your ATS with AI that understands context.
- Job-Specific Workflows: role-specific workflows with AI-assisted search, automated rubrics, smart shortlisting.
- Outreach at Scale: personalized multi-touch campaigns; AI crafts tailored messages.
- Rediscover & Evaluate: 44% of great hires already exist in your ATS. Surface silver medalists; AI scorecards evaluate against rubrics.
- Talent Insights: visualize talent pools and labor market data.

Outcomes: 2 weeks to interview-ready; 50%+ faster time-to-hire; 70% lower cost vs agencies. From kickoff to interview-ready in 14 days.

Engagement models: interview-ready candidates for specific roles (AI sources, engages, screens); dedicated AI-powered recruiting capacity (targeting 2x productivity); outcome-based pricing for complex senior roles.
