# Blue Yonder (Panasonic subsidiary) — Wikipedia

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Yonder
Fetched: 2026-08-27

Blue Yonder Group, Inc. (formerly JDA Software Group) is an American supply chain management company operating as an independent subsidiary of Panasonic Corporation. Founded 1985 (as JDA); headquartered in Scottsdale, Arizona, with an innovation center in Dallas, Texas. Revenue $1.36 billion (2024); ~8,000 employees (Oct 2024). CEO Duncan Angove.

- Following its acquisition of German AI firm Blue Yonder GmbH in 2018, JDA renamed itself Blue Yonder, Inc. (Feb 11, 2020).
- April 2021: Panasonic agreed to acquire Blue Yonder from Blackstone and New Mountain Capital for $7.1 billion (closed Sept 17, 2021). Operates under Panasonic Connect.
- 2023: acquired Doddle Parcels (UK logistics). Feb 2024: acquired flexis AG (German manufacturing/supply chain tech).
- Prior acquisitions: Manugistics (2006, >$200M), i2 Technologies (2010, $346M), Yantriks (2020).
- Blue Yonder's agentic supply chain products build on this AI/optimization heritage for retail, manufacturing, logistics and consumer goods planning.
