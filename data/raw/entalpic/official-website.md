# Entalpic

Entalpic is a Paris (France) and Montréal (Canada) based AI-for-Science startup focused on mastering fundamental chemistry towards a more efficient and sustainable future.

## High-throughput discovery engine
Entalpic's high-throughput discovery engine rapidly screens large chemical spaces to identify viable materials candidates under real industrial constraints.

## Application areas
- Thin-films and coatings where atomic-scale precision determines process yield and device reliability.
- Cathode active materials, surface coatings, and interfacial chemistries for higher-performance batteries.
- Catalytical surfaces and active sites to maximize activity, selectivity and industrial stability.
- New precursors and specialty chemicals for deposition processes (semiconductor).

## Team
Machine Learning, Chemistry and Materials experts. More than 50% PhDs. Alumni of Intel, Air Liquide, Applied Materials, Meta, Google, Amazon. Core purpose: help achieve Net Zero and drive the energy transition, working with energy-intensive industries.
