## AI for sugarcane yield optimization

Artificial Intelligence for Sugarcane Farming. The platform combines artificial
intelligence, remote sensing, and agronomic modeling to meet the specific needs of
sugarcane production and processing.

### Operational gains
- +$85 USD per 1 hectare: higher yields with the same level of inputs.
- +5% sugar yield: up to 5% increased sugar production.
- 1 tCO2 per hectare removed: aligns productivity growth with sustainability.
- 4M hectares monitored: AI powered by global data from 100% of monitored fields.

### Accuracy claims
- +97% accuracy in sugar content predictions.
- +95% accuracy in sugar content yield.
- Up to 3 million USD saved or earned on a global scale.
- Weekly projections using satellite imagery and climate data; recommendations for
  optimal harvest time; solutions for maturation and flowering.

### RenovaBio / carbon
- Up to 4.5 CBIOs more per hectare; deforestation reports; CAR analysis; geospatial
  monitoring of owned and supplier areas; automated storage and analysis of primary
  data for RenovaCalc.

### Soil moisture
- +1.5% accuracy in soil moisture readings; 14 days in advance of soil moisture
  predictions; no physical sensors required.

Gamaya operates across Brazil, India, Southern Africa, and the United States, with
more than a decade of training and testing its AI platform on sugarcane.
