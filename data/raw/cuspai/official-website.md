# CuspAI | AI-powered materials discovery

As AI transforms the physical world, new materials will open up new frontiers across semiconductors, energy and advanced manufacturing. The AI Materials Foundry brings NVIDIA accelerated computing infrastructure together with world-class chemistry and materials expertise to help power the next generation of materials discovery.

Founding members / partners of the AI Materials Foundry ecosystem include NVIDIA, Meta (FAIR), Applied Materials, imec, Hyundai Motor Group, A*STAR (Singapore), Kemira, Fujifilm, Lam Research, KIOXIA, Oxford PV, Tokyo Electron, SoftBank Corp, ICSD, Resonac, Tyndall National Institute, Wiley, Mitsui Chemicals, Universal Display Corporation, Qnity Electronics, 3M, CCDC.

Key quotes:
- Ian Buck (VP Hyperscale and HPC, NVIDIA): "The AI Materials Foundry brings NVIDIA accelerated computing infrastructure together with world-class chemistry and materials expertise."
- Rob Fergus (VP AI Research, Head of FAIR, Meta): "We're proud to be longstanding partners with CuspAI and now founding members of the AI Materials Foundry ecosystem. Our open source frontier models for materials science research will enable more teams to tackle previously intractable challenges."

Focus areas: semiconductors, energy, advanced manufacturing, carbon capture (CO2 capture materials / metal-organic frameworks), sustainable materials. CuspAI builds generative AI foundation models that search vast molecular / materials spaces and deliver viable candidate materials in a fraction of the time.
