# Flexport — AI-Powered Logistics & Freight Forwarding Platform

Source: https://www.flexport.com/
Fetched: 2026-08-27

## Positioning
"AI-powered logistics for the real world." Automate your logistics from factory floor to customer door with the world's most advanced technology platform for global trade. "Where AI-driven decision making meets real-world execution to drive logistics cost savings."

## Capabilities
- **Supply Chain Visibility**: SKU-level track-and-trace milestones for all purchase orders, syncing data across supply chain applications and orchestrating logistics execution factory-to-door.
- **Freight Forwarding Control Tower**: machine-learning-based consolidation and routing engine for air and ocean shippers; consolidates shipments in real-time, reduces international freight spend by an average of 10%.
- **Customs Brokerage**: combination of technology and licensed customs brokers; saved companies over $900M in tariffs in the last 5 years through customs entry automation, duty drawback algorithms, and trade advisory. Tools include Tariff Simulator, Tariff Refund Calculator, Product Library, and **AI Compliance Auditor**.
- **Omnichannel Fulfillment**: single pool of inventory fulfilled from network of fulfillment centers; data-driven demand planning; 99% on-time shipping, 97%+ on-time delivery.

## Notes
Flexport is a digital freight forwarder that layers AI across ocean/air/trucking freight, customs, and fulfillment. Partners include Shopify, Walmart, SHEIN Marketplace. Products span Ocean Freight, Air Freight, Trucking, Fulfillment, Trade Advisory, Working Capital.
