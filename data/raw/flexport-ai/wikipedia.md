# Flexport — Wikipedia

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flexport
Fetched: 2026-08-27

Flexport Inc. is an American multinational corporation focused on supply chain management and logistics, including order management, delivery, trade financing, insurance, freight forwarding, and customs brokerage. Headquartered in San Francisco. Revenue US$2.1 billion (2024); ~2,100 employees (2025). Founded 2013 by brothers Ryan and David Petersen.

Flexport's software integrates and connects ERP data from various companies involved in a supply chain, allowing them to manage information about cargo and streamline freight processing.

- 2022: raised $935 million in a Series E round valuing the company at $8 billion; has raised $2.3 billion since inception.
- June 2022: Amazon worldwide consumer chief Dave Clark joined as CEO (resigned Sept 2023; Ryan Petersen regained CEO).
- May 2023: Shopify sold its logistics service to Flexport in exchange for 13% of the company.
- November 2023: Flexport acquired the assets of former digital freight unicorn Convoy.
- January 2024: took on $260 million investment from Shopify.
- March 2025: WSJ reported "Freight Startup Flexport Misses Profitability Target".
