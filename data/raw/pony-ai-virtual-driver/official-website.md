# Pony.ai — Autonomous Mobility Everywhere (official website)

Source: https://www.pony.ai/
Fetched: 2026-08-27

Pony.ai is developing safe and reliable autonomous driving technology globally, having accumulated millions of kilometers in autonomous road testing in complex scenarios.

Business Units: Using the power of its core technology "virtual driver," Pony.ai is positioned across three business units: Robotaxi, Robotruck, and Personally Owned Vehicles (POV).

Global Road Tests: licenses and credentials to test and operate autonomous driving vehicles in multiple locations. Autonomous vehicles have traveled over 32 million kilometers (over 13 million miles) on urban roads and highways as of April 2024.

Our Story: Founded in 2016 in Silicon Valley, Pony.ai has become a global leader in autonomous mobility, operating Robotaxi, Robotruck and POV business units. Industry leader in the commercialization of autonomous driving. (Listed on NASDAQ: PONY.)
