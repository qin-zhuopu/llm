# Forethought — Multi-Agent System / Platform (fetched 2026-08-27)

Source: https://forethought.ai/platform/ , https://forethought.ai/customers/

Forethought's platform is a multi-agent system: Discover, Solve, Triage, Assist, Agent QA.

## Product modules
- Discover: instant insights from historical ticket data + knowledge base; auto-generates articles
  and Autoflows (agentic AI workflows).
- Solve: omnichannel agentic AI agent that fully resolves inquiries end-to-end using Autoflows
  (intent understanding, right action — no decision trees).
- Triage: classifies and prioritizes tickets; auto-tags by sentiment, language, urgency; custom
  routing models by product/region.
- Assist: agentic AI copilot for human agents in their helpdesk (auto-summaries, guided steps).
- Agent QA: evaluate 100% of interactions automatically (vs <5% manual sampling).

Reasoning engine: "Autoflows" — agentic AI that reasons through business policies to resolve
end-to-end; "Trained on Your Data" (historical data, ready day one).
