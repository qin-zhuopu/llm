## You Can't Fix What You Can't Sense

Electric utilities are expected to maintain near-perfect uptime, but they typically only learn of hazards like fallen limbs or damaged power lines after they cause outages or disasters. This gap, known as Hazard Awareness Delay, leads to expensive outages and public safety risks.

## Pinpoint Hazards - Active Grid Response

Gridware pioneered a new kind of grid intelligence: a platform that provides constant visibility into real-time electrical, physical, and environmental conditions by capturing information directly at the pole. This new class of technology is called Active Grid Response. It is defined by immediate detection, precise localization, detailed identification, and even monitoring during outages.

The Gridware Sensor (Gridscope) is mounted on distribution poles and uses onboard sensing plus machine learning to detect the small changes that precede failures - fallen limbs, damaged conductors, mechanical stress, etc.

## Developed in Partnership With Utilities

Using the platform, crews can get to the right location, with the right gear, armed with the right information. With more than 90 million field hours and counting, Gridware is already detecting the small changes that lead to big problems - preventing hazards from causing harm, helping crews restore power quickly and safely, and extending the life of assets in the field.
