## Platform Features

### Generative Search
Unlike generic search tools, AlphaSense's multi-agent research system reasons across qualitative insights, structured financial data, and your own internal knowledge to deliver accurate, auditable outputs that accelerate decision-making and eliminate uncertainty.

### Deep Research
An AI agent that functions like a team of highly-skilled analysts to autonomously generate comprehensive research outputs, build investment-grade briefings, and synthesize insights at scale — compressing weeks of analysis into minutes.

### Work Products
Build decks, reports, and tables in your firm's formats directly from Generative Search — then, extend, iterate, and polish inside PowerPoint and Excel with native add-ins that bring the power of AlphaSense directly into the apps you work in everyday.

### Enterprise Intelligence
Connect shared drives to make your memos, meeting notes, and slide decks as centralized and searchable as the 500+ million premium documents in AlphaSense's library.

### Financial Data
Comprehensive financials — including built-in screening tools, industry-specific comparables, M&A transactions, funding rounds, valuation metrics, and more. Seamless Excel integration and pre-built models accelerate analysis, while AI workflows surface strategic insights and decision-ready context.

### Workflow Agents
Turn your most critical research from a reactive, manual "pull" into a proactive, automated "push." With custom and scheduled agents, you can now build and deploy powerful AI agents to do the work for you.

### Monitoring
Customized Dashboards, tailored to your research process, bring the sources you trust together for a single view of competitors, industry trends, and macro topics, including channel-check research that Channel Check Intelligence synthesizes into concise, evidence-backed summaries. Regular Alerts keep you informed, and the mobile app lets you track insights wherever you are.
