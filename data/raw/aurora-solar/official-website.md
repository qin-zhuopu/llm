Aurora Solar: The World's #1 Solar Design Software.

One platform to sell, design, finance, and deliver solar and storage with speed, accuracy, and trust.

## Capabilities

- **Remote solar design**: Create a highly accurate solar design without having to visit the site, saving time and money during the quoting process. Uses AI and computer vision on aerial/satellite imagery to model roofs, shading, and irradiance.
- **Accurate proposals**: Create a proposal with personalized, permit-quality accuracy with just an address and an electric bill.
- **Sales engagement**: Engage homeowners with an instant, personalized 3D solar estimate.

The Aurora platform covers the most accurate, profitable way to deliver every stage of the solar product lifecycle: sell, design, finance, and deliver solar and storage.

## Context (from public knowledge / company positioning)

Aurora Solar is a US-based (San Francisco) SaaS company founded in 2013 by Stanford PhDs Christopher Hopper and Samuel Adeyemo. Its software automates solar PV system design using LIDAR, aerial imagery, and machine learning to model shading (patented Irradiance Engine), simulate energy production, and generate permit-ready designs and financial proposals. It is widely used by residential and commercial solar installers. The company has raised multiple rounds of venture funding (including a Series D) and reached unicorn valuation.
