Leading AI Recruiter for High-Volume Hiring | HeyMilo.

The AI Recruiter for teams swamped by applications. Screen and interview every applicant with structured AI voice, video, SMS, and forms. Operates 24/7 in 20+ languages. Automate screening and triage through the funnel without sacrificing quality or accuracy.

Two-way Conversational Interviews across channels: Form Screening, SMS Interview, Voice Interview, Video Interview. Example shows multilingual voice interview (agent switches to Spanish on candidate request).

Scoring: Fit score with breakdown (experience, skills, communication, availability, reliability). Score reasoning provided. Trust signals: identity verified, no cheat flags, resume matched. Human in the loop — ranked shortlist synced to ATS.

Metrics: 1.4M+ candidates screened; ~90% screening cost reduction (TRG); 80+ recruiter hours freed each week (Inergroup); 47 locations running voice screening (Neo Financial). Average candidate satisfaction 4.6/5.

Anti-cheating: active proctoring layer, an AI-classifier, and more; provides a trust score indicating likelihood of cheating.

Objective scoring: questions configured with objective evaluation criteria; every score comes with detailed reasoning; 3rd party bias audit checks (ethical AI).

ATS integrations: Greenhouse, Bullhorn, Avionté, Ashby, Workday, SmartRecruiters, JobDiva. Candidates flow in automatically; structured scores, PDF reports, transcripts, and recordings sync back. MCP server to query hiring data.

Top roles screened: Warehouse Associate, Registered Nurse, Sales Representative, CDL Driver, Support Agent.
