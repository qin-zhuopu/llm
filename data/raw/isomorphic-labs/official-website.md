### Our Goal

### Isomorphism
Our name comes from the belief that there is an underlying symmetry in the universe - between biology and information science. By harnessing AI's powerful capabilities, we can use it to model complex biological phenomena in order to help design novel molecules, anticipate how drugs will perform, and develop innovative medicines to treat and cure some of the world's most devastating diseases.
