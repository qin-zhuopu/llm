# Retrocausal — AI Copilots for Manufacturing Operations

Source: https://www.retrocausal.ai/ (fetched 2026-08-27)

Retrocausal builds AI copilots for manual assembly and manufacturing operations, using patented computer-vision and machine-learning technology to empower operators, industrial engineers, and line leaders. Named to the 2022 CB Insights Advanced Manufacturing 50 List.

## Products / capabilities
- AI Copilots that increase quality, productivity, and traceability of manual processes.
- Real-time feedback for operators + analytics for engineers and managers:
  - AI-Tracked Work Instructions and Poka Yoke (error-proofing)
  - Root Cause Analysis
  - Analytics on assembly productivity and quality
  - MES and Smart Tool Integration
  - Cycle-level Video Traceability
- Maximize Capacity: AI-powered time studies and standard work, automatic line balancing, continuous-improvement suggestions, ergonomic analyses, quality planning (P-FMEA, control plans).
- Minimize Risk: AI ergonomics assessments from smartphone video, hierarchy of controls.
- Kaizen Copilot: an LLM-powered continuous-improvement assistant (used by 3M) for root-cause analysis and driving Kaizen.
