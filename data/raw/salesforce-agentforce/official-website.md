Agentforce: The AI Agent Platform.

Agentforce is the AI agent platform that delivers 24/7 autonomous support at enterprise scale. Lose the rigid chatbots and hold times. Extend agents anywhere customers are: on the web, on the phone, or in apps. Let humans do what they do best, and let Agentforce do the rest.

Positioning: Drive outcomes, Scale service, Resolve cases.

Use cases spanning the enterprise:
- Customer Service: resolving open case, drafting follow-up email, escalating to specialist
- Contact Center: routing incoming call, pulling customer history, logging interaction notes
- Field Service: scheduling technician visit, updating work order status, confirming parts availability
- Employee Service: processing leave request, answering HR policy query, onboarding new hire
- Sales Service: qualifying inbound lead, updating opportunity, closing renewal deal
- IT Service: resolving help desk ticket, patching system alert, resetting user access

Agentforce is built on the Salesforce platform (Data Cloud, Atlas Reasoning Engine) integrating with CRM data.
