# Salesforce (Agentforce) — Wikipedia

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salesforce
Fetched: 2026-08-27

Salesforce, Inc. is an American enterprise software company headquartered in San Francisco. Traded as NYSE: CRM. Founded March 8, 1999 by Marc Benioff (chairman & CEO), Parker Harris (CTO), Dave Moellenhoff, Frank Dominguez. Revenue US$41.525 billion (FY2026); ~83,334 employees (2026). Best known for CRM software delivered via SaaS; also provides software for sales, customer service, marketing automation, e-commerce, data analytics, artificial intelligence, and agentic AI.

- September 2016: launched Einstein, an AI platform supporting several Salesforce cloud services.
- Agentic AI (Agentforce) is Salesforce's autonomous agent platform built on the Einstein/Data Cloud stack, enabling customers to deploy AI agents for service, sales, and marketing.
- Subsidiaries include Slack, Tableau, MuleSoft, Heroku, Informatica.
- Market capitalization ~US$186 billion (March 2026).
