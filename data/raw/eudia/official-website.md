# Eudia | The Enterprise Brain for In-House Legal Teams

> 来源: https://www.eudia.com
> 抓取日期: 2026-08-26

THE ENTERPRISE BRAIN — Scale how your legal experts drive enterprise growth. Eudia codifies your proprietary data and institutional knowledge into enterprise-grade legal agents.

## 核心能力

- **Institutional Knowledge**：捕获并编码政策、判例和专家推理为结构化智能
- **Proprietary Data**：连接系统、文档和工作流，确保每个决策基于实时、组织特定的上下文
- **Advanced AI**：Eudia 专有 AI，为企业决策设计，超越通用模型，对齐组织标准、上下文和判断
- **Enterprise Security**：所有智能安全、受治理、可审计，满足访问控制、合规和保护要求
