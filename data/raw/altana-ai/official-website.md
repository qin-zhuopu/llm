# Altana — The AI-Powered Network for Trusted Trade

Source: https://altana.ai/
Fetched: 2026-08-27

## Positioning
"The complexity of trade now demands the speed and scale of AI." The AI network where businesses, logistics providers, and governments are rearchitecting global trade.

## How Altana works
Altana powers **agentic trade workflows** and public-private collaboration across product value chains — the source of truth for where products come from.
- Businesses can prove compliance before goods ship and build more efficient, resilient, and secure supply chains.
- Logistics providers can automate the full brokerage workflow.
- Governments can promote economic security, manage borders, and enable the flow of trusted trade.
