# Beamery (Wikipedia)

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beamery
Fetched: 2026-08-27

Beamery (formerly Seed.Jobs) is a British human resources software company headquartered in London. It develops an AI-based platform for recruitment, talent management, workforce planning, and employee development. Founded 2013 by brothers Abakar and Sultan Saidov and Michael Paterson (ex-Goldman Sachs / Morgan Stanley). CEO Has Dosanjh. ~200 employees (2026).

Initial product applied the CRM model to recruiting: identify prospective candidates, track interactions, maintain relationships before a formal application. Software used machine learning to estimate a candidate's interest and match prospective hires with roles.

Funding history:
- Jun 2016: $2M seed (Edenred Capital Partners, Grupa Pracuj). Participated in AngelPad accelerator.
- Apr 2017: $5M Series A led by Index Ventures (~50 customers).
- Jun 2018: $28M Series B led by EQT Ventures.
- Feb 2019: Workday strategic investment via Workday Ventures; added to Workday Software Partner Program.
- Jun 2021: $138M Series C led by Ontario Teachers' Pension Plan (with Accenture Ventures, EQT Ventures, Index Ventures, M12, Workday Ventures); estimated ~$800M value; employers filled ~1 million external and internal roles through the platform in the preceding year.
- Apr 2022: acquired Flux (internal talent-mobility platform), extending into internal role matching / employee development (Beamery Grow).
- Dec 2022: $50M Series D at a $1B valuation (unicorn), total funding ~$228M.
- 2020: ranked 10th in Deloitte UK Technology Fast 50 (revenue growth 3,541% over three years); Tech Nation Future Fifty cohort.
- Jan 2023: announced ~12% workforce reduction.

Platform divided into tools for attracting candidates, managing candidate relationships, and supporting internal employee mobility.
