## Positioning
"Reinvent the Warehouse. Reimagine the Supply Chain." Organizing and orchestrating the movement of goods in our physical world. Symbotic builds robotic solutions plus AI software that "expands operational possibilities" and "sees beyond your warehouse walls."

## The Symbotic Solution — Total Warehouse Automation
Complete, end-to-end warehouse automation solutions: dense storage, maximize throughput with streamlined operations, and AI-enhanced software creating efficiency gains throughout the supply chain.

- 60–80% warehouse labor cost reduction

- 99.99%+ accuracy
- 60% storage footprint condensed
- 16x faster case processing
- 7 min time to build Smart Pallet; 42% taller Smart Pallets
- 15%+ increased shipping trailer cube utilization

## Products
- Native Case Handling — distribution facilities manage unlimited SKU volume door-to-door with predictive inventory management; goods stored in native cases, retrieved by SymBots.
- Less-Than-Case Handling (SymMicro) — each-pick / micro-fulfillment; tri-zone temperature capable (ambient, refrigerated, frozen).
