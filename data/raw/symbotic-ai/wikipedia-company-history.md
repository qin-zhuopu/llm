# Symbotic — Company & Corporate History (Wikipedia)

> Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symbotic
> Fetched: 2026-08-30
> Type: encyclopedia (secondary, aggregates WSJ/TechCrunch/Forbes/Boston Globe reporting)

Symbotic Inc. — American robotics warehouse automation company based in Wilmington, Massachusetts. Public company: Nasdaq: SYM (Class A). Founded 2007 as CasePick Systems; renamed Symbotic in January 2012. ~1,300 employees (September 30, 2023). CEO/Chairman Richard B. Cohen.

Builds and operates AI-driven automated warehouse systems for clients in the US and Canada. Software used by ~1,400 stores; customers in grocery, retail and wholesale including C&S Wholesale Grocers, Albertsons, Giant Tiger, Target, and Walmart.

## Corporate/funding milestones

- 2007: Founded as CasePick Systems.
- 2012: Renamed Symbotic.
- 2014: Target adopted Symbotic's technology instead of building a new distribution center.
- 2017: Began work with Walmart (Brooksville, FL distribution center) and Albertsons.
- Jul 2021: Walmart introducing Symbotic robots to 25 additional regional distribution centers.
- Dec 2021: Announced going public via merger with SVF Investment Corp. 3, a SPAC sponsored by an affiliate of SoftBank Investment Advisers — deal valued the company at ~$5.5B. (The Street/Forbes/The Robot Report)

## Technology

Autonomous untethered robots travel up to 25 mph, move vertically, and drop off/retrieve one case per minute — not bolted down or limited to fixed routes.

## Key backers / partners

SoftBank (SVF Investment Corp. 3 SPAC), Walmart (anchor customer and investor).
