## Risk insights for sustainable agriculture | Ceres AI

Acquire, manage and insure farmlands with confidence. Ceres AI serves agribusinesses
and financial services.

### Protect yields
Ceres is used by global farming enterprises to protect yield and increase
resource-use efficiency. Models yield impacts and ROI in ways other companies cannot,
guiding strategic investments for operations.

### Manage risk
Tools to manage climate change impacts and improve the risk profile of agricultural
portfolios. Helps farmers respond to changes in-season to protect yield and the
bottom line.

### Advance climate resiliency
More than 11 billion individual plant-level measurements in the dataset. Digitally
monitors farm management practices and creates customized sustainability scorecards.

### Team & validation
"We're farmers, data scientists, and agronomists." Data is backed by more than 30
research collaborations with leading universities, government partners, and
nonprofits.

### Notable applications
- Special report: Predicting yield loss for claims appraisals (crop insurance).
- Aerial imagery (conductance measurements) correlates with applied water; used for
  targeted fertilizer/nutrient and irrigation decisions. Flights cover more ground
  than soil moisture probes and are cheaper to implement.
