# Waabi secures $1 Billion in new funding (news)

Source: https://waabi.ai/waabi-secures-1-billion-in-new-funding-to-lead-physical-ai-revolution/
Fetched: 2026-08-27

Waabi closed its oversubscribed $750M USD Series C round co-led by Khosla Ventures and G2 Venture Partners, and secured an additional milestone-based future investment from Uber to support a new partnership to deploy robotaxis powered by the Waabi Driver exclusively on the Uber platform. The funding is the largest fundraise in Canadian history.

Founded in 2021, Waabi's Physical AI Platform combines a verifiable end-to-end AI model capable of reasoning alongside the world's most advanced neural simulator. This approach enables — for the first time in the industry — a shared brain across both autonomous trucks and robotaxis, in which the same AI model powers both applications.

Series C investors: Khosla Ventures, G2 Venture Partners, Uber, NVentures (NVIDIA's VC arm), Volvo Group Venture Capital, Porsche Automobil Holding SE, BlackRock, Radical Ventures, HarbourVest Partners, ADIA subsidiary, Linse Capital, Incharge Capital, BDC Capital's Thrive Venture Fund, EDC, TELUS Global Ventures, BMO Global Asset Management.

Uber will invest milestone-based capital to support deployment of 25,000 or more Waabi Driver-powered robotaxis over time.

"Built on NVIDIA compute, Waabi is unlocking real deployment." — Jensen Huang, CEO of NVIDIA
