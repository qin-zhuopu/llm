# Waabi crosses the next frontier of generalization (blog)

Source: https://waabi.ai/waabi-crosses-the-next-frontier-of-generalization-unlocking-truly-scalable-avs/
Fetched: 2026-08-27

Delivering on the promise of Physical AI requires systems that can generalize at human — or even superhuman — levels.

Two frontiers of generalization in self-driving:
1. Generalize across environments and behaviors — apply knowledge safely across different Operational Design Domains (ODDs), from highways to dense urban streets. The Waabi Driver is the industry's only verifiable end-to-end AI system with human-like reasoning capabilities. It reached a milestone in Q1 2025 by expanding beyond highways to complex general surface streets.
2. Generalize across embodiments — seamlessly transfer to an entirely new vehicle platform with different sensors, control systems, and physical characteristics.

The Waabi Driver was built from day one as a single AI brain designed to enable true embodiment generalization. Collaborating with Volvo Autonomous Solutions, Waabi integrated the Waabi Driver onto the Volvo VNL Autonomous. The Volvo VNL Autonomous powered by the Waabi Driver generalized zero-shot: without requiring new real-world data, simulation data, or fine-tuning, it was fully performant from the very first mile across highways and complex surface streets.

Zero-shot transfer of the Waabi Driver, trained to drive a Peterbilt 579, to a Volvo VNL Autonomous.

"For the first time in the industry, we have shown that a virtual driver can generalize across fundamentally different embodiments without requiring a single training example — neither real nor simulated — or finetuning." — Raquel Urtasun, Founder and CEO, Waabi
