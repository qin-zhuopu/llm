## About us
Our AI-first solutions combine the latest computer vision research and machine learning to
revolutionize visual assessment. Using customer images, our AI enables fast, accurate damage
appraisals, speeding up accident recovery by up to ten times.

Today, we're extending our award-winning AI to accelerate other processes, from salvaging car
parts to property assessments. Our mission is to transform how people live and work across
industries—freeing employee time for higher-value tasks, improving customer experiences,
accelerating repairs, and driving greater recycling efforts.
