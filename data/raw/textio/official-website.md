# Textio (official website)

Source: https://textio.com
Fetched: 2026-08-27

Textio – Giving feedback has never been easier.

Textio is an augmented writing / AI writing platform for HR: inclusive job descriptions and performance feedback. It analyzes language for bias and effectiveness, guiding recruiters and managers to write more inclusive job posts and higher-quality, actionable performance feedback and goals.

Positioning: augmented writing built on a large corpus of real hiring outcomes and language data; predicts how language performs (e.g., who responds to a job post) and flags biased/exclusionary phrasing in real time.
