# Polimorphic | Complete AI Platform for Local Government Service Delivery

Source: https://www.polimorphic.com
Fetched: 2026-08-27

Polimorphic offers governments practical ways to apply AI to real service problems, bridging systems without replacing tech.

## Self-service across channels
Faster than a resident can navigate through a phone tree, "Poli" can answer questions, route calls, take voicemails, or resolve common issues. Residents get fast help anytime from any device. Provides service including nights, weekends, and multilingual support.
- Colin Kennedy, City Manager, City of Newport, RI (customer).

## Workflow orchestration
Polimorphic handles requests end to end, simplifying coordination across teams and systems. Ownership is clear, submissions are error free, status remains visible, payments are accurate.
- Works across planning, public works, finance, recreation, and more.
- Eliminates silos + unifies workflows.
- Leadership gains real-time visibility across departments.
- Tooele County, UT: cut a two-day turnaround standard to half that time.

## Insights / CRM
With Poli's CRM, every question, interaction, and step of service delivery is captured in a real-time record of demand and service performance. Each client gets a dedicated Customer Success Manager.

## Traction / recognition
- Named to the inaugural AI 50 list by the Center for Public Sector AI.
- Case studies: Answering Newport (RI); "1 in 3 in New Jersey" — one in three NJ residents now has access to faster services powered by Polimorphic partnerships; Disaster Recovery in Polk County, NC after Hurricane Helene.

Poli is a closed/government-specific AI assistant (voice, text, web) built specifically for local government service delivery, with a CRM and workflow orchestration layer.
