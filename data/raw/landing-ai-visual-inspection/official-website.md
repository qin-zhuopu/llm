# LandingAI — Visual Inspection & Agentic Document Extraction

Source: https://landing.ai/ (fetched 2026-08-27)

LandingAI, founded by Andrew Ng (former Google Brain founder, Baidu Chief Scientist, Coursera co-founder), pioneered the "data-centric AI" approach to industrial visual inspection. Its flagship manufacturing product LandingLens is a computer-vision platform that lets manufacturing engineers build, deploy, and improve deep-learning defect-detection models with small datasets, focusing on curating high-quality labeled data rather than model tweaking.

Its proprietary vision models reliably extract data from complex tables, dense layouts, and multi-page documents. The system improves accuracy faster through built-in feedback and control.

## Key points
- Vision-first: proprietary vision models for defect detection and document understanding.
- Data-centric AI: accuracy improves through better, curated data; failure cases are captured, audited, and systematically fed back to reduce errors and rework.
- Agentic by design: agentic orchestration adapts to each document/inspection task — planning, deciding, and verifying until quality thresholds are met.
- Agentic Document Extraction (ADE) delivers high accuracy with confidence score and audit-ready traceability; handles thousands of pages per minute.
- Over 50+ enterprise customers; 1B+ images and documents processed; <2 second processing time.
- Enterprise security: SOC 2 Type II, GDPR & HIPAA compliant, cloud/on-prem/VPC deployment, zero data retention option.
- Modular REST APIs, Python and TypeScript libraries.

## Products
- LandingLens: end-to-end visual inspection platform for manufacturing defect detection (data labeling, model training, edge deployment).
- Agentic Document Extraction (ADE): vision-first structured-data extraction from documents.
- VisionAgent: agentic computer vision app builder.

## Manufacturing use cases
- Automated visual quality inspection on production lines.
- Defect detection with small labeled datasets.
- Edge deployment on the factory floor via LandingEdge.
