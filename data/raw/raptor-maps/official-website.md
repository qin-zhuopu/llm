## We lower risks, costs, and losses for solar

Raptor Maps helps manage more solar with less. Go from signal to action faster and more confidently with better data delivered through flexible robotic missions.

From erosion to vegetation to balance of system component defects, the platform analyzes millions of collected images to arm teams with the data they need. All insights are delivered through a map-based Digital Twin of assets, available on mobile and desktop.

## Raptor Solar Sentry (autonomous drones)

Deploy scheduled, alert-triggered, or on-demand remote missions. Remotely operated autonomous drones keep constant eyes on-site. Raptor Solar Sentry can launch missions to investigate site conditions and equipment anytime without sending anyone out. Automates time-intensive visual investigations, responds rapidly to alerts and weather events (fires, storms, SCADA alerts).

## Digital Twin

Solar plants are digitally re-constructed in a map-based interface enriched with data. The Digital Twin collects, aggregates, and normalizes data from different sources into a clean system of record. Field-tested, offline-ready RS Mobile App for technicians (works with no cell signal). Consistent robotic data capture provides a reliable time-series of inspection analytics.

## About / Founding story

Founded by two MIT engineers, Raptor Maps pioneered both drone-based aerial thermography of solar sites and delivering inspection results through interactive, map-based digital twins. At the forefront of module and equipment anomaly detection, robotics deployment on solar sites, and asset management best practices. Global engineering and technical teams support solar operations across six continents.

Leadership: Nikhil Vadhavkar (Co-founder and CEO), Eddie Obropta (Co-founder and CTO).
