# Radical AI

Radical AI: "Technological breakthroughs happen in materials. AI transforms the bottleneck into a competitive edge."

## Approach
- In self-driving labs, automation is a tiny part of the challenge — integration is what drives invention.
- "Prompts become materials." Scientists input material performance goals, then AI-generated experiments go directly to the self-driving lab, which continues refining results until it meets those goals.
- Grounded in reality: software connects directly to a self-driving lab so every idea is put to the test (rather than relying only on simulation).
- Data drives discoveries: gathering every dimension of data in a standardized format speeds analysis; feeding it back into the AI improves it.
- "Done in weeks, not years." The 10-year, $100 million process of discovering materials has held back too many technologies.

Radical AI is a materials discovery startup combining foundation-model-style AI with integrated self-driving labs (autonomous experimentation) to accelerate discovery of new materials.
