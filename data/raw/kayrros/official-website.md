## Context (from public knowledge / company positioning)

Kayrros is a French-founded environmental and energy intelligence company that applies machine learning and computer vision to satellite imagery and alternative data. It is well known for:
- Tracking global oil storage and crude inventories from satellite images of floating-roof tanks.
- Detecting and quantifying methane emissions ("super-emitter" leaks) from oil and gas infrastructure using Sentinel and other satellite data.
- Monitoring energy flows, power plant activity, and industrial output.
- Assessing physical climate risk (wildfire, flood, drought) for asset owners and financial institutions.

Customers include energy companies, commodity traders, financial institutions, and governments/regulators who use Kayrros analytics for market intelligence and emissions monitoring.
