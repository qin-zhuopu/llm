# Falkonry (now an IBM product) — IBM

Source: https://www.ibm.com/products/falkonry
Fetched: 2026-08-27

Falkonry is listed as an IBM product following IBM's acquisition of Falkonry (2024). Falkonry provides Time Series AI for industrial/operational analytics: ingest, normalize, align, enrich, analyze, and visualize time-series signals to detect anomalies and operational conditions in manufacturing and other industrial settings.

Note: As an acquired, closed commercial product now under IBM, Falkonry does not publicly disclose model parameters, architecture, or training corpus; its technology is delivered as an embedded industrial-analytics product rather than a published research model.
