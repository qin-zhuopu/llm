# Rebuy — Ecommerce Personalization Platform for Shopify

Source: https://www.rebuyengine.com/ (fetched 2026-08-27)

Rebuy is an AI-powered ecommerce personalization platform for Shopify. Claims 50,000+ Shopify brands have boosted AOV, driven conversion, and lifted LTV with Rebuy.

## Value propositions
- Improve customer experience — over 70% of customers expect brands to deliver personalized experiences; Rebuy delivers relevant recommendations across the customer journey.
- Reduce churn — personalization products boost brand loyalty and reduce abandoned carts.
- Increase AOV & LTV — Rebuy's AI accelerates revenue growth by delivering data-driven shopping experiences.

## Capabilities (product surface)
Rebuy's personalization engine powers smart cart, product recommendations, upsells and cross-sells, post-purchase offers, and search across the Shopify shopping journey, driven by shopper and catalog data.

## Positioning
Type: model system + workflow application — proprietary recommendation/personalization models over Shopify shopper behavior and catalog data, deeply integrated into the Shopify commerce stack.
