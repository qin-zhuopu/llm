# Indigo Agriculture — Funding & Corporate History (Wikipedia)

> Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indigo_Agriculture
> Fetched: 2026-08-30
> Type: encyclopedia (secondary, aggregates WSJ/Forbes/CNBC/TechCrunch/Reuters reporting)

Indigo Agriculture — Boston, MA-based agricultural technology company founded 2013 (originally "Symbiota") by Noubar Afeyan and Geoffrey von Maltzahn through Flagship Pioneering. Works with plant microbes to improve yields of cotton, wheat, corn, soybeans, rice; also offers crop storage/logistics and a carbon credit program.

## Funding history

- Founded 2013 via Flagship Pioneering.
- Jul 2016: Raised $100M ("This Bug Startup Just Raised $100 Million", Fortune).
- Dec 2016: Series D — noted as the largest private equity financing in agricultural technology sector at the time.
- Sep 2017: Raised $156M, total valuation $1.4B — became a "unicorn". (Boston Globe)
- Sep 2018: Closed Series E, bringing in $250M. (Forbes) Also acquired satellite startup TellusLabs (satellite imaging + geospatial intelligence).
- 2019: Named top company on CNBC's 2019 Disruptor 50 list. Launched carbon credits program.
- Jan 2020: Closed $200M debt round with FedEx. Raised $500M from investors including FedEx and the Alaska Permanent Fund; valuation $3.5B.
- Aug 2023: Reports indicated valuation dropped to ~$200M in a recent funding round (a ~94% drop from $3.5B). (Calcalist/Ctech)
- Raised over $300M+ in venture capital cumulatively (excluding later mega-rounds).

## Key investors

Flagship Pioneering, Alaska Permanent Fund, Baillie Gifford, Investment Corporation of Dubai, Activant Capital, FedEx.

## Products & customers

- Indigo Cotton (2016) and microbial seed treatments for wheat, corn, soybeans, rice, barley.
- Indigo Marketplace (2018): digital grain marketplace connecting growers to buyers.
- Indigo Carbon: carbon credit program. Corporate carbon buyers include Boston Consulting Group, Shopify, Barclays, JPMorgan Chase, IBM. Partners: Corteva, Anheuser-Busch, Dogfish Head, VF Corporation (The North Face), Beiselen (Germany), EMBRAPA (Brazil).
