# Blue River Technology (Deere & Company subsidiary) — Wikipedia

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Deere
Fetched: 2026-08-27

Blue River Technology is listed among the subsidiaries of Deere & Company (John Deere) on Wikipedia (alongside Bear Flag Robotics, Hagie, Monosem, Harvest Profit, etc.). Deere & Company is an American corporation manufacturing agricultural machinery and heavy equipment, traded as NYSE: DE. Revenue US$45.7 billion (2025); ~73,100 employees (2025); headquartered in Moline, Illinois.

Blue River Technology develops the See & Spray computer-vision system used in John Deere field sprayers. See & Spray uses onboard cameras and deep-learning models to distinguish crops from weeds in real time and apply herbicide only where weeds are detected (targeted spot spraying), reducing herbicide volume compared with broadcast spraying. See & Spray is commercialized as part of John Deere's precision-ag product line (field sprayers).

Note: John Deere/Blue River does not publish model parameters, architecture details, or training corpus specifics publicly; See & Spray is an embedded commercial product rather than a disclosed research model.
