## Mission
"We create intelligent machinery that solves monumental challenges for our
customers." Blue River builds advanced technology that solves complex challenges with
precision, driving productivity while reducing strain on people and the fields, job
sites, mines, and forests where they work.

## See & Spray
The flagship solution, See & Spray™, uses computer vision and machine learning to
distinguish crops from weeds. The result: less herbicide, healthier fields, and a
more resilient future for farming. "Pixel by pixel, task by task."

## Core competencies
Deep expertise across robotics, machine learning, software, and product to build
intelligent machinery that works in the real world.

## John Deere
As a proud subsidiary of John Deere, Blue River is shaping the next era of
agriculture, construction, mining, and more with breakthrough innovation at the
intersection of computer vision, robotics, and sustainability. Blue River Technology
was acquired by John Deere in 2017.
