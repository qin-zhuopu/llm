NVIDIA Research Autonomous Vehicle Research Group, led by Dr. Marco Pavone.

## Highlight — Alpamayo release

The group released **Alpamayo 1**, described as "the world's first open reasoning VLA (vision-language-action) model for autonomous-vehicle research," together with **AlpaSim**, a modular, lightweight, and data-driven perception simulator. Combined with the recently published **PhysicalAI-AV dataset**, this ecosystem delivers data, models, and closed-loop evaluation tools that enable meaningful progress toward end-to-end autonomous driving.

## Research areas relevant to Alpamayo

- Next-Generation AV Architectures: differentiable, semi-modular, and end-to-end stacks; neural and prior-augmented representations (SD maps, map priors, NeRF priors); joint prediction/planning, online mapping, neural reconstruction, policy planning.
- AV Foundation Models: vision-language foundation models, vision-language-action (VLA) modeling, scene understanding in the wild.
- Simulation: behavior/traffic modeling, language-based simulation generation, neural simulators reconstructing logged scenes and sensor data.
- AI Safety for AVs: uncertainty quantification and calibration, online monitoring of AI components, rule reasoning in neural architectures, validation (safety KPIs).

Research is developed in close collaboration with NVIDIA's AV product teams (NVIDIA DRIVE).
