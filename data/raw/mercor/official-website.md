# Mercor (official website)

Source: https://mercor.com
Fetched: 2026-08-27

Mercor | Organizing human intelligence to power the AI economy.

- Find work: Join a global network of experts solving frontier AI challenges.
- Evaluate models: Measure performance on economically valuable work.
- Hire experts: Access domain experts across 300+ professional fields.
- Transform enterprises: Turn AI into measurable business value.

Stats: Experts average contracted rate $111/hr; 411k roles created; $4M+ daily payouts. 30k+ experts: physicians, lawyers, engineers, consultants.

Core: Mercor uses AI to match domain experts to roles/projects. Originally an AI recruiting/hiring startup that automated candidate sourcing, resume evaluation, and interviewing to match talent; evolved into a marketplace supplying expert human data and evaluations to frontier AI labs. AI screens and matches candidates from resumes and interviews.

Research — APEX Benchmarks: The APEX family of benchmarks assesses whether frontier AI models can perform economically valuable tasks across professional services, medicine, and software engineering.
- APEX-Agents: long-horizon, cross-application tasks in professional services.
- APEX-Accounting: long-horizon, cross-application tasks in professional accounting.
- APEX-SWE: real-world software engineering across integration and observability.

Enterprise: Human data & evals (benchmarks and RL environments built on real professional work, APEX; human evaluation at the scale frontier labs rely on); Data monetization (automated anonymization masks 60+ categories of sensitive identifiers); Enterprise AI (map enterprise workflows, deploy production AI with Forward Deployed Engineers).
