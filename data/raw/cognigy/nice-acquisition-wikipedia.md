# Cognigy (acquired by NiCE) — Wikipedia

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NICE_Ltd.
Fetched: 2026-08-27

Cognigy is listed among the products/subsidiaries of NiCE Ltd. (Nasdaq: NICE), an Israeli-American customer-experience and contact-center software company (products include NiCE CXone, CXone Mpower, NiCE Actimize, and Cognigy). NiCE acquired Cognigy to expand its conversational/agentic AI capabilities for the contact center.

NiCE background (context for Cognigy's parent): founded 1986; HQ Hoboken, New Jersey; revenue $2.8 billion (2024); ~8,400 employees (2024); recognized by Frost & Sullivan with the 2023 Best Practices Company of the Year Award for Conversational AI.

Cognigy provides an enterprise conversational-AI / agentic-AI platform ("Cognigy.AI") for customer service voice and chat automation, now positioned within the NiCE CX portfolio ("NiCE Cognigy").

Note: Cognigy does not publicly disclose model parameters, architecture, or training corpus; its platform orchestrates LLMs and NLU rather than publishing a proprietary foundation model.
