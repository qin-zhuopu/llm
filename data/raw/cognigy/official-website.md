Enterprise Customer Service AI & AI Agents. NiCE Cognigy.

Tagline: "Say Goodbye to Poor Customer Service." Easily deploy AI Agents in your contact center to deliver fast, personalized, and exceptional service.

Positioning: Leading brands trust NiCE Cognigy to scale CX AI. Cognigy provides conversational and agentic AI for the enterprise contact center, delivering AI Agents across voice and digital channels. Cognigy was acquired by NiCE (contact-center / CX software vendor) and is integrated into NiCE's CX platform.

The platform enables enterprises to build AI Agents that handle customer service interactions in the contact center, across voice and chat, in multiple languages, with integrations into enterprise/backend systems and live-agent handoff.
