# Uptake — AI-Powered Predictive Maintenance

Source: https://www.uptake.com/ (fetched 2026-08-27)

Uptake Technologies is an industrial AI / predictive-maintenance company. It ingests fleet and asset data (sensor, telematics, maintenance records) and turns it into predictive intelligence that prevents downtime and powers performance across fleets and industrial assets.

## Key points
- Predictive analytics with external insights, compatible with major fleets.
- Real-time problem identification without touching the vehicle.
- Warranty intelligence: turn warranty data into dollars by automatically identifying and acting on eligible claims.
- One platform for every asset; standardize insights across the entire fleet.
- Alert filtering based on time, severity, location, or affected systems.

## Business
- March 2026: Bosch announced planned acquisition of Uptake, combining AI-driven predictive maintenance with Bosch's mobility, diagnostics, and telematics expertise.
- CEO: Adam McElhinney.
- Founded 2014 by Brad Keywell; was one of the highest-valued AI startups in the mid-2010s (valued ~$2.3B in 2015), backed by GreatPoint Ventures and Caterpillar.
- Customer success: United Road increased fleet uptime with 4x ROI using Uptake Fleet.

## Technical
- Machine-learning models over sensor/telematics/maintenance time-series data.
- Asset Performance Management (APM) and predictive maintenance across discrete assets and fleets.
- Real-time anomaly detection, failure prediction, and prescriptive maintenance recommendations.
- Industry heritage in an industrial data science platform (Asset IO) with failure-mode libraries.
