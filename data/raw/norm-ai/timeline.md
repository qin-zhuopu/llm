# Norm Ai — Company Timeline / Funding (fetched 2026-08-27)

Source: https://norm.ai

"Setting the Norms of Agentic Law" — Trusted by institutions managing over $35T in combined assets.

- **Norm Law**: Norm Ai's affiliated law firm, where law is practiced and encoded.
- **Supervisory AI**: The verification layer for every AI agent.

## Timeline
- 2016–2022: Foundational research on agentic law at the intersection of AI and legal theory.
- July 2023: Norm Ai founded by John Nay.
- January 2025: $48M raised from financial institutions — backed by Vanguard, Blackstone, Bain
  Capital, Citi, TIAA, and Coatue.
- September 2025: First Central Park AI Forum (convenes U.S. Senators, regulators, industry leaders).
- November 2025: Additional $50M Blackstone investment alongside launch of Norm Law — the world's
  first fully AI-native law firm for global institutional clients.
- January 2026: Mike Schmidtberger (former Chairman of Sidley Austin) joins Norm Law as Chairman
  and Partner.
- April 2026: Legal AGI Lab launches at Stanford FutureLaw (R&D arm advancing Legal AGI).
- June 2026: $120M Series C at $1.2B valuation, led by Khosla Ventures, with participation from
  Blackstone, Bain Capital Ventures (BCV), Craft Ventures, Coatue, Vanguard, New York Life, TIAA,
  Tony James (former Blackstone President/COO), Jeff Hammes (former Chairman of Kirkland & Ellis),
  and Fenwick LLP.
- July 2026: Delaware Secretary of State engaged Norm Ai to create a new company type (AIC) for AI
  agents in a regulatory sandbox.
