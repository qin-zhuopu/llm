# SymphonyAI Industrial — Industrial AI for Manufacturing

Source: https://www.symphonyai.com/industrial/ (fetched 2026-08-27)

SymphonyAI Industrial (formerly Symphony IndustrialAI / EurekaAI, part of SymphonyAI) delivers industrial AI for manufacturing that "drives real-time performance." It connects IT, OT, and edge data into one real-time system of truth to improve uptime, yield, and efficiency, spanning predictive maintenance to plant-wide optimization.

## Platform
- Unified Data Engine: ingest, transform, and operationalize every data stream — from PLCs to ERPs — turning raw industrial data into trusted, contextual insight.
- Built-in domain intelligence: templates, physics engines, and FMEAs.
- Pre-trained AI models for fast ROI, with low-code tools.
- Modular composable apps for asset performance, process, and workflows.
- "Agentic AI at the edge" — embedded in machines, integrated into processes, in the hands of frontline teams.

## Operational Intelligence (copilots)
- Natural-language copilots to interact with industrial data in plain language, surface insights, ask questions, and trigger actions.
- Context-aware recommendations from AI copilots trained on plant systems and domain data.
- Secure, governed generative AI with enterprise-grade guardrails — traceable, explainable, compliant.
- Built for OT and frontline use, embedded in industrial workflows (not chatbots).

## AI for Connected Workers
- Digital task execution with real-time guidance replacing paper procedures and tribal knowledge.
- AI-driven best-practice capture from frontline execution data.
- Smart alerts and safety triggers.
