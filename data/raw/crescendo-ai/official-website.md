Crescendo.ai: AI-native customer experience platform. Join 150+ brands.

Pillars:
- Full stack CX: One platform, one data model, one team.
- Applied expertise: deploys a team that keeps the platform calibrated to your business and owns the outcome.
- Self improving AI: AI that reviews its own conversations, finds the gaps, and updates itself for resolution that never plateaus.
- Time to value: Live in 4 weeks; high resolution within 30 days.

Specialized CX Agents working together, 24/7. Applied agents detect gaps. From requirements to validated assistant in hours. Every policy, SOP, and system connection grounded. Every conversation reviewed; misses flagged, fixed, redeployed.

Product surfaces:
- Concierge: customer-facing resolution, routing, IVR replacement
- Agent Assist: real-time assistance to human agents during live conversations
- Applied Insights: operational calibration, KPI management, cross-agent direction
- Quality: 100% of conversations scored, feeds fixes back to KB
- Workforce: forecasting, scheduling, staffing optimization

Outcomes:
- Lovepop score jumped from 3.6 to 4.3 in four weeks
- Cut email response times from 7 hours to 18 seconds
- 15% average cost savings
- <30 sec time to agent; 30% CSAT lift
- 85-86% average CSAT (Cuyana); 100% CSAT four weeks in a row; 75% CSAT increase
- Over 99.8% accuracy claim

Compliance: SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA, ISO 27001, GDPR. Tenant-isolated, audit-logged, governed by a Safe Action Framework. All channels: Chat, Messaging, Voice, Email. Seamless 24x7 handoff to human agents. QA and business insights on 100% of interactions.
