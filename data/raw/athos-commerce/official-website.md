# Athos Commerce (formerly Klevu) — AI Product Discovery Platform

Source: https://www.athoscommerce.com/ and https://www.athoscommerce.com/products/ai-agents (fetched 2026-08-27)

Athos Commerce unites Searchspring, Klevu, and Intelligent Reach under one company to transform ecommerce. It delivers ecommerce search, merchandising, and personalization plus product feed management, connecting shoppers with the right products across website, marketplaces, comparison shopping engines, social channels, and AI platforms.

## One AI engine
A single AI engine powers every agent and product in the platform, turning product and shopper behavioral data into smarter, more relevant results. Engine capabilities:
- Predictive — anticipate shopper intent and demand, optimize merchandising, promotions, recommendations.
- Semantic — understand what shoppers mean (intent and context, not just keyword match).
- Generative — create and enrich product content, descriptions, and answers automatically at scale.
- Multimodal — understand images and text together.

## AI Agents (purpose-built)
- Conversational Assistant — AI shopping guide that trains on the product catalog, web copy, and content to guide shoppers, respond to questions, understand intent, and accelerate purchase.
- Channel Assistant — audits and optimizes product feeds automatically to increase visibility on Google Shopping, expand into social commerce and marketplaces.
- GEO Assistant — enriches catalog, optimizes for Answer Engines, and syndicates to AI platforms including ChatGPT and Google Gemini (generative engine optimization).

## Built-in AI
AI woven into search (Hybrid Search: semantic AI understanding + keyword precision), merchandising, personalization, and product feed management.

## Results (self-reported)
- +951% increase in revenue per visit (Michael Stars case study)
- +15% increase in average order value

Type: model system + workflow application — proprietary AI engine (predictive/semantic/generative/multimodal) plus purpose-built AI agents for product discovery across channels. Klevu (Finland-founded AI search/discovery) is now part of Athos Commerce.
