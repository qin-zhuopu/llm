# Athos Commerce — AI & Agents

Source: https://www.athoscommerce.com/products/ai-agents (fetched 2026-08-27)

"AI built into the platform. Not bolted on." Artificial intelligence is at the core of the platform to drive smarter product discovery. One AI engine turns data into more accurate results across every channel; AI agents handle time-consuming work and make it easier for shoppers to find what they need.

## One AI engine, powering everything
Turns product and shopper data into smarter, more relevant results and improves the team's output as it continues to optimize and learn.
- Predictive — anticipate shopper intent and demand; optimize merchandising, promotions, and recommendations to improve product sales.
- Semantic — understand what shoppers mean; read intent and context, not just match keywords.
- Generative — create and enrich product content, descriptions, and answers automatically, at scale.
- Multimodal — understand images and text together, the way shoppers actually search.

## AI Agents (purpose-built for a specific role)
- Conversational Assistant — trains on your product catalog, web copy, and content to guide shoppers through discovery, respond to questions, understand intent, and accelerate every path to purchase.
- Channel Assistant — automatically audits and improves product feeds to increase visibility on Google Shopping, expand into social commerce and marketplaces, unlock incremental sales.
- GEO Assistant — enriches catalog, optimizes for Answer Engines, syndicates to AI platforms including ChatGPT and Google Gemini.

## AI woven into everyday product discovery
Built directly into search (Hybrid Search: semantic AI understanding + keyword precision), merchandising, personalization, and product feed management.
