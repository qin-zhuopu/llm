# Instrumental — Manufacturing Acceleration Platform

Source: https://instrumental.com/ (fetched 2026-08-27)

Instrumental builds a Manufacturing Acceleration Platform for complex electronics. It captures assembly data and context and turns them into insights and actions that improve yield, throughput, and ramp. Founded by former Apple engineers (Anna-Katrina Shedletsky and Samuel Weiss).

## Capabilities
- Capture Factory Signals: capture inspection, test, and process signals across builds, lines, and factories, turning them into an engineering dataset for continuous improvement.
- Discovery-Driven Inspection: AI identifies previously unknown failure modes early in production, surfacing emerging issues before they impact yield or product maturity.
- Deploy production controls: promote discovered failures into production inspection tests; deploy controls that intercept issues and synchronize inspection standards across factories.
- Accelerate Failure Analysis: saves 90% of the time spent on each failure analysis by identifying and eliminating potential root causes in minutes.

## Use cases
- New Product Introduction (NPI): meet deadlines, improve engineering efficiency, reduce unnecessary builds.
- Production control and escape prevention: reduce field failures, ramp faster, improve first-pass yield; detect functional drift across builds and lines.
- Engineering system for manufacturing: standardize production knowledge, scale faster, improve collaboration across factories.

## Metrics (self-reported customer results)
- 900+ engineering weeks saved (Meta, across 7 programs annually).
- 14 days acceleration through test & debug (NVIDIA final tray & rack assembly).
- 10% throughput increase in 30 days.
- 5.3X ROI (Toast, identifying product defects earlier in development).

## Technical
- Computer vision models over assembly-line images plus test/process signal analytics.
- AI-driven anomaly and defect discovery across builds ("discovery-driven inspection").
- Vision + multimodal AI applied to root-cause analysis and inspection deployment.
- Markets: AI compute infrastructure, high-volume electronics, aerospace and defense.
