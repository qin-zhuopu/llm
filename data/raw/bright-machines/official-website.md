# Bright Machines — Physical AI for Robotic Assembly

Source: https://www.brightmachines.com/ (fetched 2026-08-27)

Bright Machines is a next-generation manufacturer leveraging robotics and AI ("physical AI") to transform how the world designs and builds products. It focuses on software-defined manufacturing of AI infrastructure — building AI servers, AI racks, and AI storage systems using its Bright Machines Microfactories (modular robotic assembly cells) driven by AI and computer vision.

## Key points
- Software-defined design and automation capabilities; brings cloud-based streaming to factory floors (Microsoft partnership).
- Powered by NVIDIA Omniverse core technologies for AI-enabled industrial digital twins (partnership with NVIDIA).
- Bright Machines Brain: the software/AI stack orchestrating robotic assembly cells, using computer vision and ML for adaptive assembly, inspection, and closed-loop control.

## Metrics (self-reported)
- 40% faster time to revenue
- 30% lower total cost
- 15% higher reliability

## Business
- Founded 2018 (spun out of Flex); CEO Amar Hanspal.
- Raised >$430M total; announced plans to go public via SPAC (Nikola-style) at a ~$4B valuation in 2024/2025; backed by Microsoft, NVIDIA, Eclipse Ventures, BlackRock.
- Partnerships: Microsoft (cloud/streaming to factory floor), NVIDIA (Omniverse digital twins).

## Technical
- Physical AI: computer vision + machine learning for robotic manipulation, adaptive assembly, and inline inspection.
- Digital twins via NVIDIA Omniverse for simulation and virtual commissioning.
- Software-defined automation (Bright Machines Brain) that reprograms robotic cells without traditional PLC hand-coding.
- Applied to AI server / rack / storage system assembly for data-center infrastructure.
