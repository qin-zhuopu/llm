## Maximize Results with Our Digital Farming Solution | Climate FieldView

The all-in-one digital farming solution. Join the community of farmers who have
harnessed the power of Climate FieldView™.

- Data-Driven Insights: Harness your data to maximize results. Get reliable data
  right to your device so you can make informed decisions about your fields.
- Innovative Ag Tech: Explore cutting-edge hardware. FieldView adapts to keep you
  farming as efficiently as possible in every season.

### Key stats
- Most Connected Platform: FieldView's connectivity with 60+ partners makes data
  sharing easier than ever.
- Maximum Compatibility: FieldView products are compatible with most equipment types.
- +5 bu/ac: Farmers using FieldView seed scripts saw an average increase of +5 bu/ac
  compared to users who wrote their own scripts.

### Hardware
- FieldView Drive 2.0 can send seed scripts without a thumb drive, has built-in GPS,
  offers faster data syncing and compatibility with a wider range of equipment.

Climate FieldView is the digital agriculture platform of Bayer / The Climate
Corporation, covering data collection, field visualization, seed scripts, and
variable-rate prescriptions across corn and soybean farms.
