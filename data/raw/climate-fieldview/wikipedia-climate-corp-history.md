# The Climate Corporation (Climate FieldView) — Corporate & M&A History (Wikipedia)

> Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Climate_Corporation
> Fetched: 2026-08-30
> Type: encyclopedia (secondary, aggregates TechCrunch/Reuters/WSJ/New Yorker reporting)

The Climate Corporation — digital agriculture company examining weather, soil and field data to help farmers determine yield-limiting factors. Now a subsidiary of Bayer. HQ San Francisco.

## History & funding/M&A milestones

- 2006: Founded as WeatherBill by two former Google employees, David Friedberg and Siraj Khaliq.
- 2007: Raised $12.5M (weather derivatives). (TechCrunch)
- 2010: Refocused exclusively on agriculture; launched Total Weather Insurance product for corn/soybeans.
- Oct 2011: WeatherBill renamed The Climate Corporation.
- Oct 2013: Monsanto acquired the company for approximately $1.1 billion. (TechCrunch)
- Feb 2014: Merged with Monsanto's Integrated Farming System and Precision Planting divisions; acquired Solum (soil testing).
- Dec 2014: Acquired 640 Labs (Chicago), creator of the "Drive" device — later renamed the FieldView Drive — that reads tractor CANBUS data and connects to iPad/iPhone.
- Jul 2015: Sold crop insurance business to AmTrust Financial Services to focus on the digital agriculture platform.
- Jun 2018: Bayer acquired Monsanto (and with it The Climate Corporation / Climate FieldView).

## Product

Climate FieldView is the integrated digital farming platform: field visualization, seed prescription scripts, and variable-rate application prescriptions across corn and soybeans, with the FieldView Drive hardware and a 60+ partner ecosystem.
