## Satellite Agricultural Intelligence

Satellite-powered intelligence for modern agriculture, from field monitoring and
variable-rate application maps to crop identification across entire countries. Used by
140,000+ farmers and global agriculture leaders.

### For those who grow
OneSoil is a precision agriculture platform for farmers and agri-service companies who
want to improve field performance, optimize inputs, and manage operations with
confidence. It turns satellite data and AI into practical, day-to-day farming
decisions. Over 140K farmers use OneSoil every year across 180 countries.

### For those who help to grow
A growth partner for agri-service companies. Productivity zones, crop monitoring, and
predictive insights help deliver more valuable recommendations.

### AI Agronomist
"We built an AI that reads every field, every day, cross-referencing satellite
signals, weather, and crop calendar, and delivers one ranked list of what needs
attention today." Vegetation maps highlight darker zones with reduced vegetation that
may need inspection (moisture imbalance, soil compaction, nutrient deficiencies).

Collaborates with 100+ global partners. OneSoil is based in Switzerland/Belarus.
