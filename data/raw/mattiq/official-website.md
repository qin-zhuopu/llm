# Mattiq | The Data Engine for Materials

"The materials we need don't exist yet. Mattiq generates the ground truth that turns AI prediction into reality."

## Positioning
Materials AI has a reality problem. Today's models are trained largely on simulation — and inherit every approximation and bias baked into it. Mattiq grounds materials AI in experiment, generating high-throughput physical-world experimental data (ground truth) at scale.

## Material frontiers (proven in catalysis, expanding rapidly)
1. Electrochemistry: map activity and durability across million-candidate catalyst spaces — for hydrogen, fuel cells, and CO2 conversion.
2. Magnets: screen compositions for coercivity, anisotropy, and thermal stability — rare-earth-free magnets for traction motors, robotics, and defense, plus recording media.
3. Batteries: link composition and processing to ionic transport, stability, and interfacial behavior — ground truth for electrodes and electrolytes.
4. Memory: screen switching, retention, endurance, and variability — resistive, phase-change, and ferroelectric memory.
5. Superconducting Materials: map phase formation, structure, and doping across vast multinary spaces.
6. Semiconductor Materials: screen leakage, resistivity, and stability across dielectrics, oxide semiconductors, and interconnect metals.
