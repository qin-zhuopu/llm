# Mattiq — Scientific foundation (Chad Mirkin / Megalibrary), Wikipedia

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chad_Mirkin
Fetched: 2026-08-27

Mattiq's scientific founder is Professor Chad Mirkin, the George B. Rathmann professor of chemistry at Northwestern University and director of the International Institute for Nanotechnology. Mattiq's "data engine for materials" is built on Mirkin's pioneering Megalibrary technology — combinatorial synthesis and high-throughput screening of vast materials spaces to generate experimental "ground truth" for materials AI.

Mirkin background (context for Mattiq's technical provenance):
- Invented dip-pen nanolithography (DPN), Polymer Pen Lithography, Beam Pen Lithography; pioneered spherical nucleic acids (SNAs) and DNA-programmable nanoparticle assembly.
- Published over 940 manuscripts; Google Scholar H-index ~210; over 1,400 patents/patent applications.
- Elected to all three US National Academies (Sciences, Engineering, Medicine).

Mattiq application (per mattiq.com, 2026): grounds materials-AI predictions in experiment across catalysis (electrochemistry — hydrogen, fuel cells, CO2 conversion), magnets, batteries, memory, superconducting materials, and semiconductor materials. Founders/leadership include Prof. Chad Mirkin (Scientific Founder), Ben Schlatka (CEO), Dr. Andrey Ivankin (Co-Founder & CTO).

Note: Mattiq's proprietary data engine (Megalibrary-derived experimental datasets) underpins the technology, but the company does not publish model parameters or a standalone foundation model; the academic lineage (Mirkin group, published in Science) is the credible technical basis.
