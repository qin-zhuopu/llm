# Horizon Robotics — Wikipedia

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horizon_Robotics
Fetched: 2026-08-27

Horizon Robotics, Inc. (Chinese: 地平线) is a Chinese technology company headquartered in Beijing. Traded as SEHK: 9660. Founded 21 July 2015 by Yu Kai (CEO), Huang Chang, Tao Feiwen. Revenue CN¥2.38 billion (2024); ~2,078 employees (2024). The company develops AI chips used in self-driving cars and advanced driver assistance systems (ADAS). SuperDrive (HSD) is its high-level assisted-driving solution.

- December 2017: launched Journey 1.0, first generation of its in-vehicle AI chips.
- October 2022: Volkswagen Group invested $2.3 billion to establish a JV (Carizon) to develop in-house vehicle software.
- 2023: controlled 49% of the Chinese self-driving chip market (China Money Network); vehicles with Horizon chips using navigate-on-autopilot surpassed Nvidia.
- October 2024: IPO on Hong Kong Stock Exchange, priced at top of range, raising $696 million.
- January 2025: goal of shipping over 10 million chips in 2025.
- Journey 5: eight ARM Cortex-A55 CPU (26k DMIPS), GPU capable of 128 TOPS using Horizon's Bayesian BPU architecture; ISP handles 16 HD cameras; produced on TSMC 16nm FinFET; ISO 26262 ASIL-B certified (June 2021).
- Partnerships include Chery, Audi, SAIC Motor, SK Telecom, BYD, CATL.
