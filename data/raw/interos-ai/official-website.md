## Positioning
"On a Mission to Eliminate Risk From Every Supply Chain." Uncover and rank supply chain risks before they become CEO-level problems.

interos.ai uses automation and "the world's largest database of B2B relationships" to modernize how organizations manage supplier resilience. Resilient by Design — embedding proactive resilience measures into foundations rather than reacting after the fact.

Industry's first and only automated supplier resilience platform — harnesses AI to map and monitor supply chains at scale. "Get ahead of disruption — five days sooner, five moves earlier, and five layers deeper."

## Scale of the problem
Businesses only understand and monitor 2% of their supply chain, leaving 98% exposed. Opaque networks worsen disruptions costing $2.5 trillion in annual losses.

## iScore (core AI capability)
Industry-first AI-powered iScore scores extended supply chains against multiple risk factors using thousands of proprietary data points. Detects hidden ESG, Cyber, Financial, Restrictions, Geopolitical, Catastrophic and other vulnerabilities to pre-empt risk.

interos.ai established the iScore methodology as the standard by which resilient supply chains are measured, giving enterprises a way to benchmark resilience within their industry.
