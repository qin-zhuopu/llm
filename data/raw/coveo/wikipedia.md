# Coveo — Wikipedia

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coveo (fetched 2026-08-27)

Coveo Solutions Inc. is a Canadian SaaS company providing e-commerce and enterprise search software for ecommerce, customer service, digital workplaces, and websites. Publicly traded (TSX: CVO). Headquartered in Quebec City, Quebec, Canada. 500+ employees (2020).

- Founded 2005 as a spin-off of Copernic Technologies by Laurent Simoneau, Richard Tessier, and Marc Sanfaçon. Louis Têtu joined as CEO in 2008.
- Acquisitions: Tooso (July 2019, AI-based digital commerce engines), Qubit (Oct 2021, AI-powered personalization for merchandising teams).
- Funding: April 2018 $100M led by Evergreen Coast Capital; November 2019 US$172M / CAD$227M led by OMERS Private Equity, valuation US$1B+.
- Coveo Platform integrates with enterprise software: Coveo for SAP Commerce, Salesforce, ServiceNow, Microsoft Dynamics, Commerce, and Sitecore.
