# Citibot | AI Resident Engagement Solutions for Government Agencies

Source: https://www.citibot.io

The Resident-First, AI-Powered Government Communications Platform. "Empowering Government. Serving Residents."

Built specifically for government, Citibot blends enterprise-grade AI with human-centered design to ensure equity, trust, and accessibility. Residents receive verified, accurate information across every channel supported by closed-system AI that prioritizes compliance, privacy, and security.

## Platform
With over a decade of innovation, Citibot unites AI-powered Voice, Text, and Web automation to help governments deliver faster, more accessible, and trusted service — anytime, anywhere.

Key value props:
- Multichannel Convenience (Voice, Text, Web)
- 24/7 Equitable Access
- Operational Efficiency
- Deep Integrations
- Cost Savings, More Coverage

Partnering with leading governments nationwide (cities, counties). Citibot uses a "closed-system AI" designed to give residents verified, accurate answers (as opposed to open generative models that can hallucinate), tuned for the government/public-sector context (311/citizen services, service requests, notifications).
