# Optimal Dynamics — Transportation Decision System (TDS)

Source: https://www.optimaldynamics.com/
Fetched: 2026-08-27

## Positioning
"Modernize Your Truckload Operations. Automated Decisions. Real Results." The first comprehensive Transportation Decision System (TDS) designed to automatically boost utilization, productivity, and profitability across trucking organizations. "The Decision Company."

## Results (self-reported)
- 80% productivity increase: "We've been able to move 80% of our fleet managers away from routine planning tasks."
- 12.5% increase in weekly revenue per truck.

## Platform capabilities
- Real-time automated dispatch decisions — review and assign; complete a full day's plan in minutes.
- **Agentic freight procurement** across channels to proactively secure freight and improve utilization, service, and profitability.
- Bid analysis: identify best lanes to bid on specific to your network; simulate the impact of each bid, quickly identify RFPs with optimal profitability, coverage, and service.
- **Digital twin** to model scenarios before executing a single change.

## Automation at every level
Business strategy, network management, dispatching, driving (factoring driver HOS, home time, location for optimal load/tour assignment), and bid analysis.

## Notes
Optimal Dynamics commercializes decades of academic research (rooted in Princeton's CASTLE Lab, Warren Powell) on high-dimensional stochastic optimization / approximate dynamic programming applied to truckload trucking. Enterprise trucking / carrier focus.
