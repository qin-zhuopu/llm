# Nuro — Funding & Corporate History (Wikipedia)

> Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuro
> Fetched: 2026-08-30
> Type: encyclopedia (secondary, aggregates Reuters/TechCrunch/Forbes reporting)

Nuro, Inc. is an American autonomous vehicle technology company based in Mountain View, California. Founded in 2016 by Jiajun Zhu and Dave Ferguson (both ex-Waymo engineers). Since September 2024, the company has focused on licensing its proprietary Level 4 driving system, Nuro Driver, to automakers and mobility providers.

## Funding history

- Jan 2018: Launched R1 delivery vehicle with $92M funding from Greylock Partners and Gaorong Capital.
- Feb 2019: Raised $940M from SoftBank Group (Vision Fund), valuing the company at $2.7B. (Reuters)
- Nov 2020: Raised $500M Series C led by T. Rowe Price, post-money valuation $5B. (TechCrunch)
- Dec 2020: Acquired self-driving trucking startup Ike Robotics (~55 employees joined).
- Sep 2024: Business model pivot to licensing Nuro Driver technology.
- Apr 2025: Opened $106M Series E funding round.
- Aug 2025: Closed Series E with an additional $97M. Raised $203M at a $6B valuation from Uber and Nvidia plus returning backers in late-stage round. (Reuters)

## Partnerships / commercialization

- Kroger (2018), Domino's Pizza (2019), CVS Pharmacy (2020), 7-Eleven (2021), Uber 10-year partnership (2022).
- Jul 2025: Nuro + Uber + Lucid Motors partnership to launch robotaxi fleet — Uber to buy/operate Lucid Gravity SUVs outfitted with Nuro Driver; plans for ≥20,000 robotaxis over six years, first vehicle in 2026.
- Feb 2024: Collaboration with Arm Holdings for third-generation autonomous delivery vehicle.
- ~700 employees (2025).
