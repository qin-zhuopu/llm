# Applied Intuition — Wikipedia (via founder Qasar Younis)

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applied_Intuition
Fetched: 2026-08-27

Applied Intuition develops software platforms and simulation tools for physical AI, aiming to provide a common autonomy stack that can power road vehicles, industrial machines and defense systems. It cites work with 18 of the 20 largest global OEMs, autonomy startups and defense companies. Founded January 2017 by Qasar Younis (CEO, former Y Combinator COO) and Peter Ludwig (former Google PM). Marc Andreessen joined as a board member in 2017.

- As of 2025, ~1,300 employees, applications ranging from automotive driver-assistance features to military vehicles and robotics.
- Valued at $15 billion following a $600 million Series F and tender offer announced June 2025 (co-led by BlackRock-managed funds and Kleiner Perkins; new investors Franklin Templeton, Qatar Investment Authority). Up from $6 billion valuation in 2024.
- 2024: $250 million Series E at $6 billion valuation; TechCrunch reported the $6B valuation March 2024.
- 2019: raised $40 million for autonomous vehicle simulation tools (WSJ / VentureBeat).
- Investors include Andreessen Horowitz, Fidelity, Lux Capital, General Catalyst, Ray Dalio, Henry Kravis, Mary Meeker, Reid Hoffman, Mustafa Suleyman.
- "Dana" is Applied Intuition's AI copilot/assistant for vehicle software and autonomy programs.
- Sources cited by Wikipedia: Forbes (Feb 2026), Bloomberg (Jun 2025), TechCrunch (Mar 2024), WSJ (Sep 2019).
