# REP AI — Agentic Commerce Platform

Source: https://www.hellorep.ai/ (fetched 2026-08-27)

AI Agentic Commerce Platform for Growth-Focused Brands. Powering millions of shopper conversations across leading ecommerce brands.

## Problem / Solution
Shoppers browse, compare, hesitate at checkout, and leave without buying. Most stores rely on static pages, generic filters, and reactive support. REP AI makes the storefront proactive — "agentic commerce means your storefront thinks, responds, and acts." REP AI detects buying intent, starts contextual conversations, answers questions, guides decisions, and resolves support in real time across every channel.

- Detects intent — monitors hundreds of behavioral signals (hesitation, comparisons, exit risk)
- Starts conversations — contextual, natural-language responses at the right moment
- Learns and improves — every interaction becomes structured intelligence

## One AI, six tools replaced (Agentic Commerce Operating System)
1. AI sales associate — reacts to shopper behavior, narrows products through conversation, built-in Sales Skill for AI-timed complementary product recommendations, drives higher AOV with contextual upsells.
2. Support automation — resolves order tracking, returns, and policies; captures recurring product/CX issues.
3. Helpdesk — manages tickets, routing, automation, agent workflows in a unified inbox connected to the AI system.
4. Insights — analyzes every shopper interaction to uncover buying friction, product demand, and conversion opportunities.

## Multi-channel
Website interactions (chat, full-page, and search widgets with behavioral triggers), email engagement, social media (Instagram/Facebook DMs, comments, story replies), and WhatsApp — one connected AI with the same brand voice and product knowledge.
