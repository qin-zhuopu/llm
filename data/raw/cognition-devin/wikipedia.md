# Cognition AI (Wikipedia)

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cognition_AI
Fetched: 2026-08-27

Cognition AI, Inc. (also known as Cognition Labs), doing business as Cognition, is an American AI company headquartered in San Francisco. Developed Devin AI, an AI software developer.

- Founded August/November 2023 by Scott Wu (CEO), Steven Hao (CTO), Walden Yan (CPO). All three won gold medals at the International Olympiad in Informatics (IOI).
- Products: Devin AI, DeepWiki, Devin Desktop.
- ~200 employees (2026).
- March 2024: released a demo of Devin AI.
- Backed by Peter Thiel's Founders Fund ($21M early 2024, valuing at $350M).
- April 2024: Founders Fund led $175M investment, valuing at $2B (unicorn).
- March 2025: reached $4B valuation, round led by Joe Lonsdale's 8VC.
- July 2025: agreed to acquire Windsurf (agentic IDE) after Google's $2.4B acquihire of Windsurf's CEO.
- After acquisition, valued at $10B (Sept 2025), $26B by May 2026.
- Aug 2026: Bloomberg reported early discussions for new financing at a valuation of at least $40B.
- June 2026: rebranded Windsurf to Devin Desktop.
