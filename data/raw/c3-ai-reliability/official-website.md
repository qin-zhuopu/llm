# C3 AI Reliability — AI Predictive Maintenance

Source: https://www.c3.ai/products/c3-ai-reliability/ (fetched 2026-08-27)

C3 AI Reliability is C3.ai's AI-enabled predictive-maintenance application for industrial asset operators. It helps improve uptime, reduce costs, and increase productivity by unifying sensor data, maintenance records, and parts inventory to predict equipment failures and enable proactive monitoring at scale. It runs on the C3 Agentic AI Platform.

## Impact (self-reported)
- 50% reduction in downtime
- 5% improvement in overall equipment effectiveness (OEE)
- 99% reduction in alert noise
- 6 months to deploy and scale across sites

## Capabilities
- One Unified View of Asset Health: unify sensor data, documents, and process diagrams into a flexible data model for predictive analytics (C3 Agentic AI Platform).
- Predictive Failure Detection: early, accurate predictions of impending equipment risks using advanced time-series foundation models and proven ML models.
- Automated Root Cause Diagnostics: autogenerated failure-mode identification and recommendations to accelerate root cause analysis, prioritize cases, and aid maintenance scheduling.
- AI-Powered Search and Chat: bring deep context from asset history and institutional knowledge to real-time operations through embedded search and chat.
