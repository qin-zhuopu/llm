Authors: Dinkar Juyal, Harshith Padigela, ... Harsha Pokkalla, Andrew H. Beck (PathAI CEO/founder),

Submission: v1 Mon, 13 May 2024

Abstract:
Pathology is the study of microscopic inspection of tissue, and a pathology diagnosis is often the
medical gold standard to diagnose disease. Pathology images provide a unique challenge for
computer-vision-based analysis: a single pathology Whole Slide Image (WSI) is gigapixel-sized and
often contains hundreds of thousands to millions of objects of interest across multiple resolutions.
In this work, we propose PathoLogy Universal TransfOrmer (PLUTO): a light-weight pathology foundation
model (FM) that is pre-trained on a diverse dataset of 195 million image tiles collected from multiple
sites and extracts meaningful representations across multiple WSI scales that enable a large variety
of downstream pathology tasks. We design task-specific adaptation heads that utilize PLUTO's output
embeddings for tasks spanning pathology scales from subcellular to slide-scale, including instance
segmentation, tile classification, and slide-level prediction. We compare PLUTO to other
state-of-the-art methods on diverse external and internal benchmarks covering multiple biologically
relevant tasks, tissue types, resolutions, stains, and scanners. PLUTO matches or outperforms
existing task-specific baselines and pathology-specific foundation models, some of which use
orders-of-magnitude larger datasets and model sizes than PLUTO.

Key facts from paper:
- PLUTO is PathAI's pathology foundation model (light-weight Vision Transformer)
- Pre-trained on 195 million image tiles from multiple sites
- Multi-scale WSI representations (subcellular to slide-scale)
- Task-specific adaptation heads: instance segmentation, tile classification, slide-level prediction
- Matches/outperforms larger pathology FMs despite smaller data/model size
