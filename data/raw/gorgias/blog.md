# Gorgias — CX & Ecommerce Blog (fetched 2026-08-27)

Source: https://www.gorgias.com/blog

Featured article: "7 CRO A/B tests to head off support tickets before they happen" (By Nermin Canik,
with Blend Commerce). Focuses on ecommerce/Shopify customer support and conversion.

Key theme: most customer support tickets start on the product page (PDP). Six buyer questions drive
both support tickets and conversion:
1. Will it fix my problem?
2. Is it worth the price?
3. Do other people love it?
4. What's my risk if it flops?
5. How long will it take to arrive?
6. Can I get help fast?

Answering these on the PDP yields fewer pre-purchase tickets, more confident buyers, stronger
conversion, and less pressure on the Gorgias inbox.
