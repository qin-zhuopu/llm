## Resilience Solutions

Urbint is the world leader in operational resilience for energy and utilities, helping organizations predict risks, protect assets, and keep people safe. The AI-powered platform delivers three end-to-end solutions:

- **Emergency Preparedness & Response** - predicts natural disaster impacts and accelerates utility response to reduce disruption.
- **Worker Safety** - empowers crews to identify hazards and controls at the point of work; identify hazards and prevent serious injuries with predictive AI.
- **Damage Prevention** - risk-profiles 811 tickets to stop third-party strikes on underground assets; stop excavation strikes and protect critical assets before they happen.

Together, Urbint's solutions safeguard workers, infrastructure, communities, and the environment.

## Why Urbint

At the core of the approach is the "Model of the World" - a dynamic digital representation of each service territory that combines thousands of data points on weather, soil, infrastructure, population, and more. These insights power Urbint's models, enabling utilities to anticipate threats with precision and make proactive decisions that protect people, assets, and communities.
