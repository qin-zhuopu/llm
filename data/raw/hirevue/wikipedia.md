# HireVue (Wikipedia)

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HireVue
Fetched: 2026-08-27

Hirevue is an artificial intelligence (AI) and human resources management company headquartered in Sandy, Utah. Founded in 2004 by Mark Newman. ~400 employees (2023). CEO Jeremy Friedman.

The company allows clients to conduct digital interviews during hiring, where the candidate interacts with a computer instead of a human interviewer. Described as a human resources technology / "human capital management" (HCM) company and a "pioneer in the field."

Funding history:
- 2012: acquired CodeEval (adds programming challenges for hiring developers).
- Aug 2012: raised $17M Series C led by Investor Growth Capital; total $28M including B and C rounds.
- Oct 2013: raised $25M Series D led by Sequoia Capital.

Use of AI:
- 2013: began using AI to screen interviewees. Proprietary voice recognition software + licensed facial recognition software used by HireVue's Insights program.
- HireVue Insights scans applicants' verbal and facial gestures and cues, matching with skills employers seek. Assesses tone, vocabulary, and "candidate empathy". Generates an "employability" score ranked vs other applicants. ML refines accuracy over time.
- Note: In 2021, following criticism and an EPIC FTC complaint, HireVue discontinued facial analysis in its assessments (widely reported), shifting to competency/skills-based assessment.

Scale: facilitated 3 million interviews in 2015, up from 13,000 five years prior. Clients have included Nike, Starbucks, Walmart, Geico, Hasbro. Products integrate with LinkedIn and Salesforce.
