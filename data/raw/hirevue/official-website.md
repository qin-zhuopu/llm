Hiring Software | Validate skills through Video Interviewing and Assessments

Skills Intelligence — Vet, validate, verify the skills that matter. Our AI-driven platform validates skills throughout the entire hiring process.

Our AI-driven solutions streamline hiring with automated video interviewing, assessments, and conversational AI for faster, fairer, and data-driven skill validation. Hire at speed, quality, and scale with an end-to-end platform that focuses on people first.

How it works — six stages:
- Engage: Respond faster, boost recruiter productivity, connect with talent anytime with conversational AI and text automation.
- Screen: Ditch outdated phone screens. Gain data-driven insights to confidently identify and prioritize top candidates fast.
- Assess: Mitigate bias and validate skills efficiently. Combine assessments with interviews in under 20 minutes.
- Schedule: Automated interview coordination, cut time-to-hire.
- Interview: Seamless virtual interviews that sync with your ATS. Live and on-demand video interviewing at scale.
- Hire: Faster, fairer experience.

Hiring team benefits: live and on-demand video interviewing power hiring at scale; assessments identify best candidates and minimize bias; conversational AI accelerates hiring; high-quality structured interview guides improve consistency and fairness.

Candidate benefits: 24/7/365 candidate support; candidates can easily schedule interviews and complete assessments.

Security: ISO/IEC 27001:2013, SOC 2 Type 2, FedRAMP. Recurring independent third-party audits.

ATS Integration: partners with best-in-class companies for one HR ecosystem.
