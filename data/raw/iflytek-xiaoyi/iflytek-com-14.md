                            <h1 class="std_title2">2025 World University Presidents Forum Hosts "AI and Scientific Research Paradigms Shift"</h1>
                            <div class="newdet_min_ri_edr yxedr_active">
                                <p style="text-align: justify;"><b style="text-align: justify;"><span style="letter-spacing: 0px;">The 2025 World University Presidents Forum</span></b><b style="text-align: justify;"><span style="letter-spacing: 0px;">&nbsp;(WUPF)</span></b><b style="text-align: justify;"><span style="letter-spacing: 0px;">, co-hosted by the China </span></b><b style="text-align: justify;"><span style="letter-spacing: 0px;">Association of </span></b><b style="text-align: justify;"><span style="letter-spacing: 0px;">Higher Education and Zhejiang University, was held </span></b><span style="text-align: justify; letter-spacing: 0px;">on November 8 </span><b style="text-align: justify;"><span style="letter-spacing: 0px;">at the Hangzhou International Expo Center. The event received support from the UNESCO Regional Office for East Asia (UNESCO Beijing) and the World Federation of United Nations Associations (WFUNA), with co-sponsorship from the National Academy of Education Administration, the Open University of China, Higher Education Press, and </span></b><b style="text-align: justify;"><span style="letter-spacing: 0px;">iFLYTEK</span></b><b style="text-align: justify;"><span style="letter-spacing: 0px;">.</span></b><span style="text-align: justify; letter-spacing: 0px;">&nbsp;Focusing on the theme &quot;Artificial Intelligence and the Future of Higher Education,&quot; the forum brought together nearly 1,000 representatives, including 167 guests from over 40 countries and regions across five continents. With its formidable capabilities in data processing and analysis, AI efficiently manages massive, high-dimensional datasets, enabling the focused allocation of scientific resources to address critical issues vital to human development. </span><span style="letter-spacing: 0;font-size: 16px">Higher education institutions are crucial to the deep </span><span style="letter-spacing: 0;font-size: 16px">integration of AI into scientific research. In response, China&#39;s Ministry of Education has implemented a series of initiatives to advance talent cultivation, technological innovation, research and development (R&amp;D), and the commercialization </span><span style="letter-spacing: 0;font-size: 16px">and application </span><span style="letter-spacing: 0;font-size: 16px">of research outcomes. To maximize the impact of higher education in navigating this transformation of research paradigms, it is essential to pool the collective wisdom of universities worldwide and the unified efforts of all sectors of society.
