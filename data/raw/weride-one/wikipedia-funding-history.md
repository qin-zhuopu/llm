# WeRide — Funding, IPO & Deployment History (Wikipedia)

> Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WeRide
> Fetched: 2026-08-30
> Type: encyclopedia (secondary, aggregates Reuters/TechCrunch/CNBC/Bloomberg reporting)

WeRide Inc. (文远知行) — autonomous driving technology company founded 2017 in Silicon Valley by Tony Han (former Chief Scientist of Baidu's Autonomous Driving Unit). Public company: Nasdaq: WRD, SEHK: 800. Holds driverless permits in 8 countries (China, US, UAE, Saudi Arabia, Belgium, Switzerland, Singapore, France). Vehicles deployed across 60+ cities in 13 countries. Over 2000 employees as of 2025.

## Funding history

- Sep 2017: $52M pre-A round led by Qiming Venture Partners, Sinovation Ventures, Nvidia and others.
- Oct 2018: Series A led by Renault-Nissan-Mitsubishi Alliance.
- Jan 2021: $310M Series B (after B2/B3); $200M from Yutong Group (lead); Qiming, Sinovation, Kinzon Capital participated. (TechCrunch/Reuters)
- May 2021: $310M Series C at $3.3B valuation; investors IDG Capital, Hechuang, CoStone Capital, Cypress Star, Sky9 Capital, K3 Ventures, CMC Capital, Qiming, AlpView. (TechCrunch)
- Dec 2021: Strategic investment from GAC Group.
- Jan 2022: Series D led by China-UAE joint sovereign fund, Asia Investment Capital, Bosch, GAC Group, Carlyle Investment Group — valuation $4.4B.
- Nov 2022: Series D+ led by China Development Bank Capital — post-money valuation $5.1B.
- Oct 2024: IPO on Nasdaq (ticker WRD), raising $439.9M; first publicly listed universal autonomous driving / Robotaxi company. Filed at ~$5B valuation. (Reuters/Bloomberg)
- Mar 2025: Filed application to Hong Kong Stock Exchange for secondary listing.

## Key investors

Qiming Venture Partners, Sinovation Ventures, Nvidia, Renault-Nissan-Mitsubishi Alliance, Yutong Group, IDG Capital, CoStone Capital, Sky9 Capital, K3 Ventures, GAC Group, Bosch, Carlyle Investment Group, China Development Bank Capital.

## Commercialization

- Products: Robotaxi, Robobus, Robovan (Robovan W5), Robosweeper S1.
- Uber partnership: robotaxis on Uber platform in UAE (Abu Dhabi first fully driverless commercial ops Nov 2025), Dubai (Apr 2026), Saudi Arabia (Riyadh), Switzerland (Zurich, 2026).
- Renault Group / beti / Macif partnership: first European fully driverless Robobus in Drôme, France (Feb 2025).
- Mass production: Chery, GAC AION.
