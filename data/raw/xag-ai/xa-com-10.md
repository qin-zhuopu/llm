    <div class="faq common-title ft2 fw-700" >Frequently Asked Questions</div>
    <ul class="faq-list" >
      <li>
        <span>• What does it mean when the app says "Unable to take off (reserved)?"</span>
        <div class="collpase-content" >
          <div class="main-content" >
            <div>It indicates that the drone has detected insufficient flight conditions:</div>
            <div>1. It may be due to a bad environment. It is recommended to check whether the UAV's positioning system<span>has entered RTK mode prior to takeoff, and then perform the self-check test on the motor system.</span></div>
            
            <div>2. If the UAV still cannot take off after passing the self-check, it is recommended to change the take-off<span>point, restart the drone and the companion app, and upload the route again.</span></div>
            
          </div>
          <span class="bg" ></span>
        </div>
      </li>
      <li>
        <span>• Why does the XMission indicate camera missing or SD card missing?</span>
        <div class="collpase-content" >
          <div class="main-content" >
            <div>1. Check whether the firmware version is updated to the latest version, then restart the APP and the XMission.</div>
            <div>2. Gently wipe the socket with a soft rubber eraser and replace with another camera or SD card (the XMission is a highly integrated device, so you MUST power it off before attempting to change the parts. Keeping the machine on while swapping parts is STRICTLY PROHIBITED, as it will lead to severe damages to the PCB).</div>
            <div>3. Clean up the camera's cache and restart the XMission and its app.</div>
            <div>(If you still cannot solve the problem after trying the above steps, please return the equipment to the factory for inspection and repair.)</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </li>
      <li>
        <span>• Why can't the activation code be scanned so that I can activate my device?</span>
        <div class="collpase-content" >
          <div class="main-content" >
            <div>Try logging in to the following URL and manually enter the code to activate your product: <a target="_blank" href="https://agri.xaircraft.com/pm/#!/main" >https://agri.xaircraft.com/pm/#!/main</a></div>
            <div>(If you still cannot activate the device, please provide the account, password and the picture containing the QR code to the dealer or XAG's staff, who will then assist you.)</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </li>
      <li>
        <span>• Why is my battery locked? I need help unlock it.</span>
        <div class="collpase-content" >
          <div class="main-content" >
            <div>Batteries can be unlocked remotely, but the following conditions must be met:</div>
            <div>1. Make sure that the battery is charged fully or to a sufficient amount.</div>
            <div>2. Place the battery on top of the drone.</div>
            <div>3. Make sure the drone is connected to the Internet (the "cloud" logo on the top of the app turns blue, indicating that the drone is online. However, since some countries and regions do not support cloud communications, drones used wherein cannot connect to the Internet and cannot be remotely unlocked).</div>
            <div>4. When the above conditions are met, please send the serial number of the battery to XAG staff requesting an unlock over-the-air.</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </li>
      <li>
        <span>• What are the recommended settings for spraying height, speed, and droplet size, and spray width?</span>
        <div class="collpase-content" >
          <div class="main-content" >
            <div>We recommend a spraying height of 2-3m (optimal 2.5m), a spraying speed of 5-7m/s, a droplet size of 110 microns, and a width of 4m.</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </li>
      <li>
        <span>• Why can't the motor of the spray drone rotate, and it feels a lot of resistance trying to manually rotate it?</span>
        <div class="collpase-content" >
          <div class="main-content" >
            <div>1. It may be that the three-phase wire terminal of the ESC bottom board is short-circuited, and the three-phase wire terminal needs to be expanded normally. You can use a tool to increase the distance between the three terminals during installation.</div>
            <div>2. It may be the problem of the motor or the connecting wire. Check whether the related circuit of the motor is connected normally. You can exchange it with another motor accessory and cross-check.</div>
          </div>
        </div>
        
      </li>
      <li>
        <span>• Why does the R150 App show "Please release the single-hand controller. (Error code 65543)</span>
