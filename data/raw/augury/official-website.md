# Augury — Machine Health & Process Health AI

Source: https://www.augury.com/ (fetched 2026-08-27)

Augury is an industrial AI company specializing in Machine Health and Process Health for manufacturing. It combines proprietary vibration, temperature, magnetic, and ultrasonic sensors (Halo) with machine-learning diagnostic models to predict mechanical failures on rotating equipment (motors, pumps, fans, compressors) before they cause unplanned downtime.

## Key points
- 1.1B+ hours of real machine readings power its diagnostic models, agents, and notifications, continuously contextualizing data across the operation.
- Positions against "generic AI chats" with "context-aware agents that act" — AI agents that put operational and machine knowledge to work.
- 15+ years of building industrial diagnostic solutions.
- Machine Health: predictive maintenance for rotating machines using edge sensors + ML.
- Process Health: analytics for the production process to reduce waste and improve throughput.

## Business
- Reached unicorn status; raised a Series F of $75M in 2023 at a valuation over $1B (total raised >$500M).
- Customers include large manufacturers such as Colgate-Palmolive, DuPont, Hershey, and Roseburg.
- Diagnostics are backed by a hybrid AI + human "diagnostic engineer" verification loop, giving high-confidence, low-false-positive alerts.

## Technical
- Proprietary multi-modal edge sensors (vibration, temperature, magnetic field, ultrasound).
- Machine-learning classifiers trained on the world's largest labeled dataset of machine-failure signatures.
- Diagnostics delivered via SaaS dashboard, alerts, and increasingly through agentic assistants.
