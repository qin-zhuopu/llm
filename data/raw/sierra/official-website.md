## Meet Sierra — The leading conversational AI platform for businesses

Upload SOPs, transcripts, whiteboard photos, and audio recordings—or explain your goal in plain English. Ghostwriter builds a production-ready, multilingual, multichannel agent—with built-in guardrails.

Automate agent updates based on flagged issues and proactive insights, with full visibility into every change—so you can review, validate, and ship with confidence.
