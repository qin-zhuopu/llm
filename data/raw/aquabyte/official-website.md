## Simplifying decision-making in fish farming

Market leading technology that drives data-driven decision-making in fish farming,
providing the most accurate datasets on welfare, lice, behavior, and growth in
aquaculture.

### Key numbers
- 1.3 million daily images per camera
- Over 800 systems in pens
- 8 years of data collection

### The Aquabyte solution
Decision support powered by over one million images per day and artificial
intelligence. Data provides fish farmers with insights into their fish and the
environment, enabling better decision-making throughout the entire production cycle.
Continuous data collection and analysis make it easier to track developments over
time, detect changes at an early stage, and take action when needed. Enables a more
proactive approach to fish welfare, production, and resource utilization.

### Hardware
- HYDRA 360: both a fish health camera and a feeding camera. Equipped with seven
  integrated custom lenses positioned around the camera, providing a 360-degree view.
  Self-cleaning lenses, integrated sonar and environmental sensors, compact all-in-one
  solution for fish health monitoring and feeding operations.
- Hammerhead camera: engineered for submerged pens; self-cleaning lenses, multiple
  sensors, compact size, low weight.

### Story
Founded in 2017 with a vision to harness AI and computer vision to transform
aquaculture. Started in Norway's salmon industry, grew into a global company trusted
by fish farmers in all major markets. Technology evolved from weight estimation and
automatic lice counting to a comprehensive platform delivering a full suite of data
and insights (welfare, lice, behavior, growth).
