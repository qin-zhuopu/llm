The Agentic Dialog Platform. The enterprise platform where dialog agents get built, run, adapt, and iterate in real time.

Built for complexity. Dialog agents proven on the hardest conversations in the world, including fraud, outage, triage, multilingual disputes, and more.

Reported customer outcomes:
- Health insurance provider: CSAT boost from day one
- Hotel and casino brand: $1.2M revenue from one voice agent

The Agentic Dialog Platform: One platform to build, run, adapt, and govern dialog agents at enterprise scale, for every builder.
- Two ways to build: Poly Agent Builder for non-technical teams or ADK for developers, both with the same dialog-native runtime underneath.
- Dialog agents, full stack: Not a chatbot. Not voice bolted onto chat. A full-stack dialog agent built specifically for enterprise customer engagement, and powered by their proprietary model trained on 1B+ enterprise conversations, called Raven.

PolyAI 成立于 2017 年，创始人 Nikola Mrkšić（CEO）、Shawn Wen（CTO）、Eddy Su 均为剑桥大学机器学习研究者。公司专注企业级语音 AI，处理最复杂的客服对话（欺诈、故障、分诊、多语言纠纷）。其对话模型基于 10 亿+ 真实对话训练（专有模型称 Raven）。投资方包括 Khosla Ventures、Georgian、Passion Capital。据 Forrester 口径 ROI 显著。已发表学术论文被引用超 12,000 次。办公室分布在纽约、伦敦、旧金山、贝尔格莱德、多伦多。
